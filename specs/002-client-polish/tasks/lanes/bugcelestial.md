## Lane 002-lane-bugcelestial (T32200+)

Operator report from the try14 play: "clouds render (good); sun and moon have a big black square around them" (screenshots 13.30.07, 13.30.15). Based on lane bugsky's head 532beb9f (it adds client/src/sky-render.cob). No existing open task covers it: T30505 (lane bugsky) records "the OVERLAY blend of the sun, moon and stars (the shim has alpha blend only)" as not done; this lane is the client-side approximation of it.

- [x] T32200 [US3-client] (lane bugcelestial) Root cause (client, not core, not shim; shim untouched): vanilla draws the sun, moon and end flash sprites with the additive OVERLAY blend (12 section 3.4c: SRC_ALPHA, ONE), so the sprite's opaque black background adds nothing; the shim has SRC_ALPHA / ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA only, so the generated vanilla pack's 32 x 32 sprites (corner pixel 0 0 0 alpha 255; every pixel opaque) painted a black quad. Red first: `client/tests/acc_sky_celestial_alpha.cob` (generated vanilla pack's real sun, full moon and new moon sprites on the block atlas: black corner alpha 0, sun centre 255 255 217 opaque, glow pixel hue at full brightness with its brightness as alpha, a block sprite untouched) failed 5 of 7 (`expected=00000000 observed=000000FF`); `client/tests/test_atlas_additive.cob` (7 cases) failed to link before ATLAS-ADDITIVE existed. Fix: `client/src/atlas.cob` ATLAS-ADDITIVE (colour = c x 255 / max(r, g, b), alpha = max x alpha / 255, both rounded) applied by ATLAS-BUILD to the still images of `minecraft:sun`, `minecraft:moon/*` and `minecraft:end_flash` before they are copied into the atlas (padding included), so SRC_ALPHA blending adds the pixel's rgb x alpha and dims the sky only by that brightness.
- [ ] T32201 [US3-client] (lane bugcelestial) NOT DONE, noted: the stars (flat swatch quads of the UI atlas, no sprite) have no black square; vanilla adds b x b to the sky where the alpha blend lerps towards white by alpha b (brighter at dusk and dawn). The built-in pack's flat swatch fallback for the sun and the moon is an opaque tinted square (no art; T30504). An animated celestial image (a pack's `.mcmeta`) is not transformed. A true additive blend needs a shim pipeline (the OVERLAY blend: the GLINT pipeline's colour factors are SRC_COLOR / ONE).

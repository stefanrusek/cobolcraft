## Lane 002-lane-villagers (T12100+)

Operator report on the release build: (1) villagers never take a profession (a composter next to an unemployed villager does nothing); (2) the trade dialog does not open on a right click of a trader or villager.

- [x] T12100 Villager professions in the running game (44 sections 1.3, 2.1, 2.5, 2.6, 19 sections 22.3 and 22.7, 46 section 6.2). Causes: (a) the POI index was filled by one section of one chunk per 5 ticks per villager, the 49 chunks round it in turn, so the first chunk needed 24 x 245 ticks (about five minutes) and a chunk was never scanned again, so a workstation placed after that was never indexed (the 2-block fallback scan of VL-SCAN found only a site within 2 blocks at the instant of its 20 to 39 tick check); (b) a claimed potential job site was walked to only in the work activity and only to 3 blocks, while the profession is taken within 2 (AssignProfessionFromJobSite); (c) VillagerData (Set Entity Data index 19: type, profession id, level) was never sent, so the client could not see the profession. Fixes: WORLD-SET-BLOCK indexes a placed point of interest at once (`PXI-NOTE`), the scan goes on with the chunk in progress, then the villager's own chunk, then the 7 x 7 round; the potential job site is walked to in the idle, work and play activities up to 2 (squared); MOB-DATA-VALUES / MOB-DATA-BUILD send index 19 (`VIL-PROF-ID`, 46 section 6.2) once it differs from plains / none / level 1, and `MOB-DATA-SEND` follows a taken or reset profession. Test: `core/tests/acc_us15_villager_job_chain.cob` (CORE-TICK only; red before: a composter 4 blocks away and the index 19 entry; green: a composter next to the villager makes it a farmer, the Set Entity Data carries type 2, profession 5, level 1, a second villager walks to a second composter 4 blocks off and becomes a farmer).
- [x] T12101 Trade dialog, server (40-menus-5 section 2.1, 19 sections 22.4 and 23): the core path already opened the merchant menu (Interact 0x1A on a villager with offers and on a wandering trader: Open Screen 0x3C type 19, Merchant Offers 0x35); an unemployed villager and a nitwit open nothing (correct). Test: `core/tests/acc_us15_merchant_interact.cob` (the real Interact packet; the farmer takes its profession from a composter and CORE-TICK first).
- [x] T12102 Trade dialog, client: the cause of "nothing opens". The client never sent Interact (0x1A); a right click on an entity sent nothing (only Use Item On for a block, Attack for the left button). `CLP-INTERACT` (protocol-play.cob) and `CONTROLS-SEND-INTERACT` (controls.cob): the right button pressed on the world screen with a mob or player aimed at sends Interact (main hand, the entity id, the interaction point 0 relative to the entity, the client's sneak flag). The aimed hit point is not tracked, so the point is the entity's own position (a use that reads the click height, such as an armor stand's slot, gets 0). Test: `client/tests/acc_us15_villager_interact.cob` (red before: nothing sent; green: `1a14000000` for entity 20; out of reach nothing).
- [x] T12103 Regression and coverage: `make cover-changed SINCE=origin/002-client-polish ONLY="acc_us15_villager_job_chain acc_us15_merchant_interact acc_us15_villager_interact"` reports 0 uncovered changed lines; `make lint` clean; the villager, merchant, trader, mob metadata and controls test families pass.

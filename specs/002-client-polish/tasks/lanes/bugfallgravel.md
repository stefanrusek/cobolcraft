## Phase bugfallgravel (lane bugfallgravel, operator report of db901a9a: "gravel still deletes torches, but it doesn't delete lanterns; both should break the falling gravel"; T58800-T58899)

Operator report: gravel (or sand) falling onto a torch deletes the torch; onto a lantern it does not delete it, but the gravel then sits on top of the lantern. Both should break the falling block. Reference: docs/protocol/29b part 1 sections 3.1, 4.2 steps 2 and 4, 4.3 steps 4 to 6 and 4.5; 51-block-shapes R-C17 (lantern shapes), R-D1 (carpet); 24 part 3 (snow layer replace rule); 29 section 9.5 (`DirectionalPlaceContext`). Related lane: bugfallblock (torch fix, merged into this branch from 002-lane-bugfallblock; db901a9a did not contain it, which is why the operator still saw torches deleted).

What the docs say: a torch (and wall, soul, redstone, copper torch) has no collision shape, so the falling block flies into its cell; a lantern has a collision shape (standing 9 pixels high, hanging 10), so the falling block comes to rest on top of it, inside the lantern's cell. In both cases the entity's block position is the torch's or the lantern's cell (4.2 steps 2 and 4), neither is replaceable (the `replaceable` flag is false; 4.3 step 4 reads `canBeReplaced`), so the block is not placed: step 6 discards the entity and spawns the block's own item (gate: game rule `entity_drops`), and the torch or lantern stays untouched. The operator's recollection is right for both.

- [x] T58800 [bugfallgravel] Root cause (core, `FB-STEP`, mob-21-8.cob; the lantern half; the torch half is bugfallblock's `FB-CELL-FREE`): the move used `ENTITY-BOX-HITS`, whole solid cells (`CB-SOLID`), so a block with a partial collision shape (lantern, carpet, snow layers, slabs, fences) counted as a full block: the falling block stopped on the cell's top face, one cell too high (`WS-TOP + 1`), and was placed ABOVE the lantern or carpet, no item. The docs' rule (the entity rests on the real top of the shape, inside the cell) was never reached.
- [x] T58801 [bugfallgravel] `FB-BOX-HITS` (new, mob-21-8.cob): the falling block's collision against the collision SHAPES of the cells (`BSH-COLL-ID` tables of `BSH-BUILD`, 51-block-shapes; the stand-in set's blocks are full cubes by `CB-SOLID`, as movement.cob), hit and top y as a coordinate (lantern cell + 0.5625, full block cell + 1); the cell below the box is scanned too (a fence's shape is 1.5 high). `FB-STEP` uses it for the three axis moves and lands at the shape top, so its block position is the cell it ended in. `TNT-STEP`, ground items and mobs keep `ENTITY-BOX-HITS` (not this lane).
- [x] T58802 [bugfallgravel] `FB-STEP` spawns the "cannot be placed" item at the entity's position after the move (4.5: the entity's position), not where the tick began (the falling block's previous position, up to a block higher).
- [x] T58803 [bugfallgravel] `FB-CELL-FREE`: a snow layer is replaceable (the `canBeReplaced(context)` of the landing) only when `layers` is 1 (24 part 3, snow: the held item is empty, not snow); `isFree` keeps the flag, so a block over deeper snow still falls into it and is refused there. The flag alone is already true for every layer, so without this rule three layers of snow would be overwritten once the shapes let the block reach the cell (one layer has no collision shape, 51-block-shapes, and is replaced by the falling block; before the shape change a one-layer snow counted as a solid cell whose own replaceable flag made it a free floor, so gravel over it dropped as an item instead of taking the cell).

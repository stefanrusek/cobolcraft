## Phase bugtorch (lane bugtorch, operator report of try13: torches on walls; T30000-T30199)

Operator report: (1) "I can no longer click on a wall to place a torch on it; previously it didn't attach but I could place one." (2) "Torches on walls don't give off any light, so the stronghold is completely dark." Related open work: CC-0009 (block placement orientation and attachment, draft ticket); this lane does the torch family of it only.

- [x] T30000 [bugtorch] Root cause 1 (core): since T10305 (`SUP-PLACE-OK`, bugsw) a torch placed through Use Item On must survive where it goes, and the item placed the STANDING torch state on whatever face was clicked (`BLOCK-PLACE` took `IT-PLACES-STATE` as is), so a torch clicked on the side of a wall had no block under it and was refused; before T10305 the same click put an unattached standing torch in the air. The torch items are standing-and-wall items (25 part 4 section 9.2) and nothing implemented the wall half of them.
- [x] T30001 [bugtorch] `core/src/torch-place.cob` (new): `TORCH-WALL-BLOCK` (torch, soul_torch, redstone_torch, copper_torch to their wall blocks) and `TORCH-PLACE-STATE`: 25-4 section 9.2 with the attachment direction DOWN, the nearest looking directions of 29 section 9.5 with the opposite of the clicked face first (`UI-NEAREST`, `UI-NEAREST-DIRS`, written for 29 section 9 and tested but not wired), the wall state the first horizontal direction whose wall torch (facing the opposite of it) survives (24 part 3 section 7.8 `WallTorchBlock.getStateForPlacement`), the result the first direction other than UP whose candidate survives (standing for DOWN, wall for a horizontal one); nothing survives: the placement is refused and the item stays. `BLOCK-PLACE` (blocks.cob) calls it after the replaceable and body checks.
- [x] T30002 [bugtorch] `SUP-OPEN` (support-29.cob): `copper_torch` and `copper_wall_torch` get the standing and the wall rule like the other torches (they had no survive rule at all, so a copper torch was placed in the air and stayed).
- [x] T30003 [bugtorch] Test `core/tests/acc_torch_standard.cob` (superflat world of the generated standard content set, skipped as a pass without CC_GENERATED_DIR): torch, soul_torch, redstone_torch and copper_torch clicked on the north, south, west and east faces of a block with nothing under the target cell become the wall torch of their kind facing the clicked side (item used up, block light 14, 10, 7, 14); the top face gives the standing torch; the underside of a floating block places nothing and keeps the item. Red before (all 16 placements refused or standing, copper standing in the air, no light), green after.
- [x] T30010 [bugtorch] Root cause 2a (core, `BLOCKS-FILL`): the per-state light of the block table read only a leading number and the text "lit, else 0", so every other state-dependent text of the light column of the generated rows was 0 or wrong: redstone torches and redstone wall torches (`lit?7:0`), redstone ore (`lit?9:0`), smokers and blast furnaces (`lit ? 13 : 0`), campfires (`lit ? 15 : 0`), soul campfires, copper bulbs (15, 12, 8, 4 when lit), sea pickles, respawn anchors, light blocks, trial spawners and vaults emitted nothing; candles gave 3 whatever their count (3 x candles); a furnace gave 13 lit or not; cave vines and their plant gave 14 without berries; glow lichen without a face gave 7. `BLOCKS-LIGHT-KIND` (kind of each text) and `BLOCKS-STATE-LIGHT` (light of a state from its properties) in block-props.cob. wall_torch itself (14), soul_wall_torch (10) and copper_wall_torch (14) were plain numbers and were right.
- [x] T30011 [bugtorch] Test `core/tests/test_block_light_states.cob` (generated standard set, skipped as a pass without CC_GENERATED_DIR): 92 states of the emitters named above and a set of constant emitters against the light column of docs/protocol/24 (every state is named by all of its properties). Red before (38 of 92 failed), green after.
- [x] T30020 [bugtorch] Root cause 2b (core, `WORLD-CHUNK-LOAD`): a chunk the standard generator made counted no light sources (`CK-SOURCES` is what the light engine scans: only sections with a count above 0 are searched for sources), except through overflow written after the fact and a marker; a chunk read from a region file counted them (`CHUNK-NBT-READ`). So the wall torches, lava, glowstone and every other emitter a freshly generated chunk held gave no light until the chunk had been saved and read back, and the stronghold's torches (written by the generator) never lit it. `CHUNK-COUNT-SOURCES` (chunk-data.cob) counts the emitting cells of a chunk by section; `CHUNK-NBT-READ` and `WORLD-CHUNK-LOAD` (generated chunk) call it.
- [x] T30021 [bugtorch] Test `core/tests/acc_gen_light_standard.cob` (standard world, seed 12345, the start chunk of the nearest stronghold and the eight chunks around it; skipped as a pass without CC_GENERATED_DIR): the emitting cells of each chunk equal the sum of its `CK-SOURCES`, every generated wall torch lights its cell with at least 14, and the same after the start chunk is saved, released and read back from its region file.
- [x] T30030 [bugtorch] Client: no change. The client keeps the light that Chunk Data and Light Update packets carry (`protocol-client.cob`), has no emission table and no placement prediction (Use Item On is sent for any block aimed at: `controls.cob`), so both symptoms were the server's.
- [ ] T30040 [bugtorch] Not done, same family (CC-0009): standing signs, ladders, levers, buttons, banners and tripwire hooks are still placed by `BLOCK-PLACE` with the default state of the item (facing north, floor), so on a wall they are refused or attach to the wrong side; the torch items are the only standing-and-wall items wired to the placement rules of 25 part 4 section 9.2.

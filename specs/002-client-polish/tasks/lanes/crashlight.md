## Lane 002-lane-crashlight (T5700+)

- [x] T5700 Operator hang while a creeper blew up (the process ended with SIGTERM, libcob prints that as "termination (signal)", not a crash: the operator stopped a frozen game; the innermost frame WG-FIND is only where the time went, GEN-DATA-READY runs for every chunk the light spread enters). Root cause: a block change redid the block light of the whole 3 x 3 neighbourhood from every light source in it (`LIGHT-BLOCK-CHANGED` calling `LIGHT-BLOCK` for nine chunks, each spreading every source of nine chunks through a 31-cell window): a change near a lava lake or a lit room took minutes, an explosion changes dozens of blocks. Measured (debug): 32 glowstone blocks placed in a row 11 s (0.35 s each, growing with the sources), 256 in 1.7 s after the fix. `WORLD-CHUNK-PEEK` was already a pure read for a sliced dimension (it never queues generation; `GEN-QUEUE-SLICED` only reads the loaded terrain data), the light spread is iterative with a bounded queue, and the block-break path (`BLOCK-CHANGE`, `LIGHT-SET-BLOCK`) is the same code as the explosion path, so both are fixed together. Test `core/tests/acc_crashlight.cob` (standard world, real generated data, skipped as a pass without it): 256 light sources placed, a block change and an explosion at the border of the loaded area load no chunk and finish in seconds.
- [x] T5701 `LIGHT-BLOCK-CHANGED USING row, world x, cell y, world z` redoes only the cells within 14 steps of the change (the 4089 cells of taxicab distance 0 to 14: a path through the changed cell that matters is at most 14 long, and so are both halves of it): their light is cleared, seeded from their own sources and from the light of the cells just outside (unchanged, exact), and relaxed from level 15 down with one bucket list per level (a cell is final when first popped), so the cost no longer depends on the number of sources. Loaded chunks of the neighbourhood that were not lit are lit first (`LIGHT-CHUNK`), chunks that are not loaded are never loaded and block the light. `WORLD-SET-BLOCK` passes the position. Test `core/tests/test_light_window.cob`: after 22 changes (sources and walls across chunk borders, on the lowest and highest layer) the nine chunks equal a full `LIGHT-BLOCK` recomputation; a chunk with no loaded neighbour is lit and nothing is loaded; an unlit loaded neighbour is lit. Line coverage of `LIGHT-BLOCK-CHANGED` is 100 % from these tests.
- [x] T5702 `LIGHT-CHUNK` lights a chunk from its own sources and the light along the borders of the lit neighbours (`LIGHT-FRESH`: one relaxation from level 15 down over the 48 x 48 cells around the chunk, raise only, so the light it gives to lit neighbours is pushed in the same pass); loaded neighbours that were not lit are lit first, unloaded ones never loaded by a sliced dimension. Test `test_light_window`: nine chunks lit one at a time equal the full `LIGHT-BLOCK` recomputation; a chunk of 10240 light sources is lit in 0.12 s (debug).
- [x] T5703 Incremental block light (operator's design, matches vanilla's BFS): `LIGHT-BLOCK-CHANGED` no longer redoes a fixed window; the changed cell takes the larger of its emission and (when not opaque) its brightest neighbour less one; brighter than before: `LIGHT-ADD` raises neighbours only where their stored level is lower (work = cells that change); dimmer: `LIGHT-REMOVE` (two-phase: cells lit through it are cleared to their own emission, cells of equal or higher level are re-queued) and then `LIGHT-ADD`. `LIGHT-FRESH` (T5702) uses the same `LIGHT-ADD`; shared work area `core/copy/LIGHTWORK.cpy` (`LW-CELLS` counts the cells touched). Chunks not lit or not loaded stay out of a change and take the light when lit. Counting test (`test_light_window`): first light 7392 cells, adjacent second 4089, a wall in the dark 1 cell, removal 8182; the nine chunks equal the full `LIGHT-BLOCK` recomputation after each change. Timings (release): 256 light sources placed one by one 219 s before, 0.77 s now; an explosion in that field 48.5 s before, 0.27 s now; a chunk of 10240 sources is lit in 0.12 s (debug), so no per-tick budget is needed. Open: a bounded per-tick queue for chunk lighting is not built (not needed at these numbers); `LIGHT-BLOCK`/`LIGHT-SPREAD` remain as the full-recompute reference used by tests. Existing `test_blocks` now sets its neighbour unlit explicitly (lighting a chunk lights its loaded neighbours). `acc_us12_drowning` fails on the base branch as well (not caused by this lane).

## Phase buglevelnum (lane buglevelnum, try-db901a9a play-test bug: after levelling up by trading with a villager the number for the level renders incorrectly; T58700-T58799)

- [x] T58700 [buglevelnum] Root cause (client only; the Set Experience decode `client/src/protocol-client.cob` is right: progress float, level VarInt, total VarInt, and the HUD draws the level, not the total): `UI-TEXT-C` coloured a text by a grey brightness (`RL-UI-DARK`, the luminance of the colour), but the UI pipeline ignores vertex brightness (shim-abi section 9, `shim/shaders/ui.vert`: texel x tint), so every coloured text was drawn white: the four black outline copies of the level number came out white too, merging with the white centre into one white blob (operator screenshots 2026-11-19_09.01.57 and 09.00.13, scale 4). The same fault whitened every text with a colour (tooltip lines, inventory titles in #404040, enchant costs). Fix: `UI-TEXT-C` sets the tint (`RL-UI-TINT`) to the colour for the glyph quads and clears it; `RL-UI-DARK` is gone (CLSTATE, UI-QUAD, UI-RENDER). Tests: `client/tests/test_hud_xp_text.cob` (new: levels 7, 12, 100, 1000 at GUI scales 1 to 4; first glyph of each of the five copies at widget position x scale, four copies tinted (0, 0, 1) and the green (128, 255, 32), five copies of one quad per digit, centred (W - width) / 2, y = H - 35) and `test_ui_text_tooltip.cob` (the colour is the tint 0.251, not a brightness), red before the fix.
- [x] T58701 [buglevelnum] The green constant of the level number and of the experience bar's fallback fill was 8454176 (0x810020, a dark red) instead of #80ff20 = 8453920 (12 section 5): `client/src/screens/hud.cob`, `client/src/ui-render.cob` (the progress swatch when the pack has no `hud/experience_bar_progress`), `client/tests/test_hud_xp.cob` (the old constant was asserted; three of its cases failed red with the right one).
- [x] T58702 [buglevelnum] Checked, no change needed: the experience bar fill (background 182 x 5 at ((W - 182) / 2, H - 29), progress clipped to (int)(progress x 183) pixels at most 182, `test_hud_xp`), the level number's position H - 35, centring and digit count for levels above 99 (T58700 test), and no number at level 0 or in creative.

## Lane 002-lane-slabwater (T6000+)

Operator play of the try3 build on a skyblock world: "the sides of blocks that are surrounded by lower half slabs don't render", and "fall damage after slowly descending in a water stream".

- [x] T6000 Face-level occlusion in the mesher (12: `Block.shouldRenderFace` compares the face occlusion shapes; 24 part 1 section 5.2 occlusionShapesByFace; 51 outline shapes): the cull test was "the neighbour is opaque" (a full cube; every slab, stair, carpet and trapdoor by class was not), so a double slab was no occluder and a slab hid nothing, not even its own side against the full block beside it. `client/src/state-faces.cob` turns the outline shape of every state that occludes by its shape (class rule of STATE-ROW-OPAQUE, now with slabs, stairs, carpets, snow layers and trapdoors in; flags noOcclusion, noCollision, replaceable still out) into a 16 x 16 mask per side, in two readings (the sixteenths its boxes touch for its own face, the ones they cover wholly for what it hides); equal masks share a class (52 in the standard set, at most ST-CMAX 300) and `ST-COVER` says which class hides which. The mesher decides a face with three table reads (ST-NB of the neighbour, ST-OWN of the cell, ST-COVER): a neighbour hides a face only when its face toward it covers the whole face, so a full block beside a bottom slab draws its side, the slab's side against the full block is hidden, a bottom slab against a top slab draws both, a double slab is opaque. The same-block rule (glass against glass) is now `ST-LIKE`: a state that does not occlude but has the full cube outline (a trapdoor or a fence beside its like is drawn). Tests: `client/tests/test_mesher_faces.cob` (brute-force oracle over one chunk of air, stone and every dry state of oak_slab, oak_stairs, oak_trapdoor, white_carpet and snow; the expected face is computed from the shape boxes; the operator's case), `test_mesher.cob` sets ST-NB for its hand-made opaque states. Open: snow layers and trapdoors stay non-occluders (flags), so a layer of eight hides nothing.
- [x] T6001 Fall damage after sinking through water (29a 8.3 step 2 resetFallDistance, 17 section 4.11): FLUID-TOUCHES-WATER (core/src/breath.cob, read by MOVE-PLAYER and the mobs) looked at the column of the feet's centre only and at water blocks only, so a player sinking along the edge of a stream (the 0.6 wide box inside the water, the centre over a dry cell) kept its fall start and took the damage of the whole drop on landing. It now scans every cell of the box (0.6 wide, shrunk by 0.001, feet to 1.8) and counts a water block of any level (source, flowing, falling), a waterlogged block (a source) and a bubble column. The client keeps no fall state (the server decides damage), so its prediction is not involved. Test: `core/tests/test_fall_water.cob` (20 blocks through a falling-water column, red before: beside the column with the box reaching into it, 18 health lost; the same drop through air hurts; waterlogged slab and bubble column wet, a dry slab not).

Operator report on try3: "my underground save is still super laggy" (4 fps with F3 open underground; the surface ran about 35 fps).

## Lane 002-lane-nethperf (T6300+; T6200 to T6230 are the poses lane's)

Operator report on try5: entering the Nether through a portal printed "went to minecraft:the_nether" and then the game seemed hung for well over 60 s; once in, about 17 fps with stalls to 1 fps for a few seconds. Measured on the release build (scratchpad/nethperf/bench: 625 chunks = view distance 12, fixture of the real generated packs): a Nether chunk costs 75 ms of generation (terrain 55 to 60, features 10, biomes) and 30 ms of light with its neighbours loaded; the join and every move of a Nether player sent the whole view or row inside one tick (`CHUNK-FEED-ON` was the time-sliced standard pipeline only), and the light of each sent chunk generated its eight neighbours inline (`WORLD-CHUNK-PEEK`). Before: 70.7 s on the main thread for the 625 chunks, one chunk send up to 800 ms, one tick up to 290 ms (scratchpad/nethperf/bench/before-nw.txt).

- [x] T6300 The Nether's chunks are made by the generation workers: `GEN-WORKER-HANDLE` makes a Nether request by `WORLDGEN-DIM-CHUNK` over empty memory, `GEN-QUEUE-PICK-MODE` counts the Nether in the workers' share, `WORLDGEN-DIM-CHUNK` takes a worker's finished answer for a Nether chunk (`GEN-STANDARD-TAKE`) before generating it, `GEN-WORKER-NETHER` (pool active and Nether generator), and `WORLD-CHUNK-PEEK` does not generate a Nether light neighbour while the workers serve it. Test `core/tests/test_nether_worker.cob` (red: scratchpad/nethperf/red.txt, link failure of WORLD-LIGHT-READY).
- [x] T6301 The Nether is fed like the standard overworld (`CHUNK-FEED-ON`): a join, a dimension switch or a move no longer sends the view in one tick, the queue and workers make the chunks over the following ticks, nearest first; a chunk is sent only when its 3 x 3 neighbourhood is loaded (`WORLD-LIGHT-READY` queues the missing ones, so the light never generates them inline) and `CHUNKS-UNLOAD` keeps a Nether chunk one further out for that light. The tests that expected the 25 chunks inside the switch (`test_nether_player`, `test_portal_39_8`) expect them later. After (release, same 625 chunks): 3 workers: main thread 12.9 s in all (about 20 ms a chunk), longest tick 40 ms, longest send 60 ms; no workers: longest send 60 ms, longest tick 300 ms (two chunks a tick, 71 s in all but spread over 365 ticks instead of one) (after-w3.txt, after-nw.txt).
- [ ] T6302 Open: the portal itself still generates the 3 x 3 chunks round the exit inline (`PORTAL-CREATE` reads the 1089 columns through `WORLD-GET-BLOCK`; 1.9 s once, measured), a block read cannot be deferred without moving the search; the client's own frame cost in the Nether (17 fps, lava and emissive meshes, mesh credit) was not profiled (no window here).

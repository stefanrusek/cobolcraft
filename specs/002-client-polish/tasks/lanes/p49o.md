## Lane p49o (T36000-T36499)

Scope: the player-visible creation paths of the entities lane p49f built: /summon of every entity type the core can create (with the NBT argument applied through the entity loaders), the tracker sends, the entity type tab-completion list, natural bat spawning, spawn eggs, and /kill, /data get entity and /tp on the new types. Each line names the test that was red before the change.

- [x] T36000 [US15-core] (lane p49o) /summon of the non-mob entity types (16 §5.8, 21 §8, 45-8, 45-9): test `core/tests/test_cmd_summon_entities.cob` (147 cases; red: every family answered entity.not_summonable, 125 failures observed). `SUMMON-FAMILY` / `SUMMON-CAN` / `SUMMON-SPAWN` (core/src/summon.cob) create ground items, experience orbs, primed TNT, falling blocks (`FB-SPAWN-AT`, the cell is not touched), area effect clouds, lightning bolts, end crystals, armor stands, item and glow item frames, paintings, the seven minecarts, boats and rafts, interaction, block and item display, mannequin, cushion and marker entities, arrows, snowballs, eggs, ender pearls, experience bottles, fireballs, small fireballs, shulker bullets, llama spit, wind and breeze wind charges, splash and lingering potions, ominous item spawners, firework rockets, eyes of ender, the wither and the ender dragon; the viewers are sent each (the marker, tracking range 0, is not); player and fishing_bobber (noSummon) and the leash knot (no entity in the core) stay entity.not_summonable. Missing: no creation program or no document for text_display (the text component default), spectral_arrow, trident, evoker_fangs, wither_skull, dragon_fireball, breeze, the leash knot entity, and the mobs of the standard set that have no row (axolotl, camel_husk, copper_golem, creaking, drowned, elder_guardian, giant, guardian, nautilus, skeleton_horse, sulfur_cube, warden, zombie_horse, zombie_nautilus): they stay entity.not_summonable.

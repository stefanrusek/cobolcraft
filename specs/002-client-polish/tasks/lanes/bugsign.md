## Phase bugsign (lane bugsign, operator report of try15: "can't edit sign text"; T43000-T43199)

Operator report: signs now place correctly on posts and walls, but the text editor does not open or does not save. Vanilla flow (31 part 3 sections 2.5 to 2.9, 05a 0x3D and 0x06, 05c 0x3E, 40 part 8 sections 1.2 and 6): placing a sign opens the editor for the placer (front side); right-clicking a sign opens it for the side the player faces unless the sign is waxed; Sign Update stores four lines under the editor lock; Block Entity Data goes to the viewers; honeycomb waxes, dye colours, a glow ink sac makes the text glow, an ink sac takes the glow away.

- [x] T43000 [bugsign] Root cause (core): the sign rules existed as a tested library (`core/src/be-31-3.cob`: `BE3-SIGN-USE-EMPTY`, `BE3-SIGN-USE-ITEM`, `BE3-SIGN-UPDATE`, `BE3-SIGN-FACING`, `BE3-SIGN-TICK`) that nothing called. (a) `PLAY-DECODE` did not decode Sign Update (0x3E, id 62) and `WS-LATER` did not list it, so `PLAY-PACKET` treated it as a bad packet and CLOSED THE CONNECTION when the client sent the text; (b) nothing sent Open Sign Editor (0x3D) on placing a sign or on a right click (`BLOCK-USE-ON` had no sign branch); (c) nothing sent Block Entity Data (0x06) after a change (`BE-DATA-PACKET` had no live caller, `BC-BROADCAST` sends block updates only). The client's editor screen and its Sign Update sender (`signscreen.cob`, `textedit.cob`, tested by `test_screen_40_8_sign`) were fine.
- [x] T43001 [bugsign] `core/copy/PLAYPKT.cpy`, `core/src/play.cob`: Sign Update decoded (`PSB-BX/BY/BZ`, `PSB-SIGN-LINE` of four byte lengths and texts of at most 384 bytes, side VarInt in `PSB-DATA`; a line above 384 code units, a cut-short packet and trailing bytes are malformed) and routed to `SIGN-UPDATE-PACKET`.
- [x] T43002 [bugsign] `core/src/sign-edit.cob` (new): `SIGN-USE` (called from `BLOCK-USE-ON` before the use rules choose: a sign takes the click; a sneaking player holding an item still places; an applicator is tried first (`SIGN-ITEM-KIND`: honeycomb, ink sac, glow ink sac, `<colour>_dye`), one item is used up in survival, Block Entity Data goes out; otherwise the empty-hand path opens the editor for the side the player faces (`SIGN-FACE-SIDE`: 31-3 section 2.4, rotation x 22.5 degrees or the facing's yaw, a wall sign's hitbox centre on the plate side); `SIGN-PLACED` (called from `BLOCK-PLACE`: the placer's editor opens for the front side, 2.6 `setPlacedBy`); `SIGN-OPEN-EDITOR` (Block Update, then Open Sign Editor with position and side VarInt); `SIGN-UPDATE-PACKET` (2.8: stored only under the editor lock, then Block Entity Data); `SIGN-SEND` (`BE-DATA-PACKET` to the players who have the chunk in view); `SIGN-LOCK-TICK` (2.9, called by `BE-TICK-ALL` in furnace.cob: the lock ends when its player is gone or farther than 4.0).

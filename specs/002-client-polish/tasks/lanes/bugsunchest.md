## Lane 002-lane-bugsunchest (T62200-T62299)

Operator report from the try19 play, with reference screenshots of regular Minecraft next to CobolCraft: (1) a closed chest's lid top shows a dark recessed rectangle with a lighter border, as if the chest were open; (2) the sun is a gold square with an orange rim and a lighter inner square, where vanilla has a pure white square with a soft pale blue halo. No existing open task covers either (T5001 baked the chest; T32200 made the celestial sprites alpha-blend).

- [x] T62200 [US3-client] (lane bugsunchest) Chest lid top. Root cause (client, `client/src/models-chest.cob` CHEST-BAKE face table; not core, not shim): doc 52 open question 1 was settled by the earlier implementer by exchanging the two ends of y so the side strips read upright, which also put the DOWN rectangle (u + d, v) of the unwrap, the dark inside of the lid, on the geometric top and the UP rectangle (u + d + w, v), the planks, on the bottom. The pack's chest textures (read from the generated vanilla pack's pixels) have the planks in the UP rectangle for the lid and the black inside in the UP rectangle of the base, so the top of every cuboid reads the UP rectangle and the bottom the DOWN rectangle; the side faces are right and stay as they are. Fix: the two face rows take each other's texture rectangle (corner order and winding unchanged). Red first: `client/tests/test_chest_lid_top.cob` (real pack through CC_GENERATED_DIR, pixel under the middle of the lid top, the lid underside and the base bottom for 13 chests: single in four facings, left and right halves of the chest and the trapped chest, the ender chest, the copper chest and an exposed copper half) failed 51 of 87 cases (the lid top sampled `432700FF`, the dark inside, for the chest; the underside showed the planks) before the fix.
- [x] T62201 [US3-client] (lane bugsunchest) Sun colour. Root cause (client, `client/src/atlas.cob`, bake of the celestials atlas sprites; not the sky draw and not the pack lookup: the pack's `textures/environment/celestial/sun.png` IS drawn): vanilla adds the sun sprite to the sky (12 section 3.4c, OVERLAY blend) and the sum saturates: the sprite's gold rim (255, 213, 74) and pale centre (255, 255, 217) become pure white over the sky colour #78a7ff, and the halo pixels only lighten the blue. The shim's alpha blend cannot clamp a sum, and T32200's bake (colour = rgb scaled to full brightness, alpha = brightness) therefore drew the sprite's own colours opaque: a gold square with an orange rim. New program `ATLAS-ADDITIVE-DAY` bakes the sun only against the day sky: white, alpha = the mean of the red share r / 135 and the green share g / 88 (each at most 1) times the pixel's alpha; the moon and the end flash keep `ATLAS-ADDITIVE` (they are seen over a dark sky, where the old bake is exact). Tests: `client/tests/test_atlas_additive_day.cob` (7 pixels), the sun cases of `client/tests/acc_sky_celestial_alpha.cob` (real pack: corner `FFFFFF00`, centre and rim `FFFFFFFF`, halo `FFFFFF22`; before the fix the centre was `FFFFD9FF`).

The reference screenshot of vanilla has no clouds in frame, so no difference was shown. The sunrise/sunset glow fan is still open (T30505).

## Lane 002-lane-bugnighttrade (T59600 to T59699)

Operator bug (try17 play): "villagers don't trade at night".

- [x] T59600 [US15-core] Established from the docs (19 sections 22.4, 22.8, 23): `mobInteract` ignores the click only for a dead, already trading or sleeping villager (a baby is refused with the head shake; a villager without offers shakes its head). There is no daytime rule, so an awake villager trades at any world time; a villager asleep in its bed does not. Core reads the same way: `MERCHANT-INTERACT` (menu-merchant.cob) refuses only `VL-SLEEP = 1` and a baby; the client's `CONTROLS-SEND-INTERACT` has no time test; nothing in the villager brain strips a profession or sets a "rest" block on interaction at night.
- [x] T59601 [US15-core] Root cause, class (b): `VL-BRAIN` (mob-vil-brain.cob) sets `VL-SLEEP` as soon as the villager is within 2 blocks of its claimed bed at rest time (12000 to 9 of the next day) but leaves it standing where it was, upright, 1 to 2 blocks off the bed, and the generic stroll of the mob step could still walk it about while it was flagged asleep. There is no sleeping pose on the wire either, so from the player's seat the villager is awake and refuses the trade. 19 section 22.8: `startSleeping` puts the villager at the bed position. Fix: at sleep start the villager is moved to the middle of the bed block at the mattress height (`ENTITY-MOVE`, y + 9/16, 51 R-E3), and while it sleeps the stroll is held off (cooldown 1, destination its own cell). Test (written first): `core/tests/acc_bugnighttrade_interact.cob` (Interact packet; night 14000, 22900 and 18000 awake in the open and under a roof trades, asleep in the bed refuses and lies on the bed, trades again in the morning).

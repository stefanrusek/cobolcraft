### Report B: walls (and fences, panes, bars) do not connect

- [x] T62800 [bugfluidwalls] Root cause (core, not the client): nothing in the core computed the sides of a wall, fence, glass pane or iron bars. `PLACE-STATE` left the default state (post, no arm) and `BU-HOOK` kind 4 (updateShape) had no rule for them, so a placed block never connected to anything and never changed when a neighbour did. The client was checked first (T62803) and draws every state it is sent. The operator's "fences definitely update" is not what the code did either: the same defect, a fence, a pane or iron bars placed beside a block stayed unconnected (the fences of generated structures carry the states of their templates, a likely reason those looked right).
- [x] T62801 [bugfluidwalls] Acceptance test `core/tests/acc_bugfluidwalls_connect.cob` (red first: 28 of 36 checks failed before the fix; standard world, generated pack, the packets the client sends, then Level.setBlock with its neighbour updates): wall beside wall (both sides low, post kept), a straight run (no post), a corner, beside stone (sturdy face), leaves, pumpkin and a shulker box (exception blocks), a bottom slab (not sturdy) and a double slab, iron bars and glass panes, a gate across and a gate along (axis rule), a stone block above (tall arms), a wall above (post), the same changes made after the placement (updateShape from a horizontal neighbour, from above and from below), another wall kind beside a mossy wall, fences (same wood class, nether brick fence, gate, leaves, stone), panes (stone, fence, other pane) and bars (wall).
- [x] T62802 [bugfluidwalls] `core/src/connect-29.cob` (new; docs 24-7 sections 8.3 and 8.4, 24-3 section 9.1, 24-4 section 5.2, 24-5 section 7.14): `CONN-KIND`, `CONN-EXCEPTION`, `CONN-FACE-GRID` (the face of a collision shape as sixteenths, from BSH-COLL: sturdy = all 256 covered; the down face of the block above for tall arms and the post), `CONN-CONNECTS`, `CONN-COMPUTE` (sides none/low/tall and the post by shouldRaisePost, booleans for the others, the wall_post_override tag), `CONN-PLACE` (called at the end of `PLACE-STATE`), `CONN-SHAPE` (called by `BU-HOOK` kind 4 before the support rule: a horizontal change recomputes that side only, UP only for walls, DOWN none).

### Report A: the fountain's water is drawn on the wrong sides

- [x] T62810 [bugfluidwalls] Root cause (client, `MESH-FLUID`): a fluid quad has its corners in the order of the renderer (counter-clockwise from outside, the normal pointing out of the cell) but the translucent sections are drawn with the back faces culled (T10401, `CC_PIPE_TRANSLUCENT_CULL`), where a front face has its first triangle pointing into the cell as the baked block faces do. Every water side and bottom was therefore a back face seen from outside: only the far faces of a column (through the water, from the inside) showed, a block away from where the water is. The winding test of T10401 for a water cell compared a face centre with a whole number, but the fluid faces are inset by 0.001, so it checked nothing. Reproduced by the stronghold fountain scene (36-5 type 1: pillar of stone bricks, source on top, four falling columns, pool): 36 side faces, none a front face.
- [x] T62811 [bugfluidwalls] Test `client/tests/test_mesher_fluid_winding.cob` (red first: 5 of 8 cases failed; real pack): the fountain scene (36 side faces as front faces, 36 copies for the inside, nine surfaces seen from above and from below), a source in the air, under a solid ceiling (no copy of the surface for below), beside glass (the overlay face single-sided) and a lava cell (every face once).
- [x] T62812 [bugfluidwalls] `client/src/mesher-fluid.cob`: the front copy of every face has its corners reversed (`WS-REV`), the copy for the inside is the renderer's order; made for the top when `shouldRenderBackwardUpFace` holds (29a section 10.3: a cell of the 3 x 3 above that is neither the same fluid nor a solid block; before, always), for a side that does not take the overlay sprite (29a 10.3: drawn on both sides; before, never), never for the bottom (single-sided) and only for translucent sprites (lava is drawn by the opaque pipeline, which does not cull). `FLUID-OUT` holds up to eleven quads (`CLFLUID.cpy`). `client/tests/test_mesher_ice.cob`: the water cell with the missing (opaque) sprite has six quads.
- [ ] T62813 [bugfluidwalls] Not done (gaps named in the head of `mesher-fluid.cob`): the partial-shape occlusion of a neighbour (`isFaceOccludedByState` for slabs, stairs and the like: the client has no shape of a state, an opaque neighbour hides and any other does not) and the corner height rule for a solid but not opaque neighbour (glass counts 0.0, the renderer counts -1.0).

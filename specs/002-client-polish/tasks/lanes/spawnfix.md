## Lane 002-lane-spawnfix (T6800+)

- [x] T6800 Hostile mobs spawned in daylight, also in a lit forest and at once in a new world (operator report). Root cause: `MS6-RULE-AT` (core/src/mob-18-6.cob) read the light with `STDMOB-LIGHTS`, the raw stored light, and the stand-in light policy stores sky light 0 at and below a column's topmost non-void block, so a spawn cell that holds grass, a fern or a flower, or lies under leaves or glass, read as sky 0 and block 0, which passes `isDarkEnoughToSpawn` (18 section 6.8) in full daylight. The rule itself (sky above the nextInt(32) draw, then the sky light less the night's darkening against the 0 to 7 level) was already as documented, and an open-air cell was already rejected by day. `MS6-RULE-AT` now calls `LIGHT-RAW`, which lights such a cell by the sky when no opaque block is above it (the policy `growth-29b-1b` already uses); a cave under an opaque roof still reads dark. Test `core/tests/test_mob_18_6_light.cob`: red before (grass in the cell 200 of 200 draws pass, under a leaf canopy 200 of 200), green after.
- [x] T6801 Spawn protection around the world spawn: 18 section 6.5 step 2 (with 44 part 2 section 3) rejects a candidate within 24 blocks of the level's respawn position when that position is in the dimension; `MS6-PACK` only tested the distance to the players. It now rejects a candidate whose squared distance to the centre of the spawn block is below 576 in the first enabled dimension (the one the spawn point lives in). Same test: no pack lands within 24 blocks of the world spawn (red before: 189 spawned in 80 tries) and the same pack spawns at night when the spawn is far away.
- [x] T6802 Cases kept green in the same test: no spawn by day under open sky, one in a cave (stone roof) by day, one at night under open sky; the 24-block distance to the players stays covered by `test_mob_18_6_5`.
- [x] T6803 Open, not changed here: the spider's daylight neutrality (`STDMOB-AGGRO`, light 13 or more at the cell above its feet) still reads the raw stored light (`STDMOB-LIGHTS`); it is correct on bare ground, and a spider standing under leaves or glass by day would read dark and stay hostile. `STDMOB-LIGHTS` itself is shared with mob-20-10 and the burn test, so it was left alone.

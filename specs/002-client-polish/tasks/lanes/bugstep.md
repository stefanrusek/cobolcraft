## Lane 002-lane-bugstep (T58600-T58699; operator report on try16: "movement across block height differences that should not cause problems blocks movement and the screen shakes, like 2 pieces of logic are fighting; it hits a lot in villages but it exists everywhere")

Root cause (client, not core): the client's collision box for the local player was hard-coded to the cobolcraft stand-in operator (0.7 wide, 1.75 high; `PHYS-BOX`, `PHYS-POSE` in client/src/physics.cob), while the core's movement check (`MOVE-COLLIDES-POSE`, core/src/movement.cob) takes the box from the content set's player entity: 0.6 x 1.8 for a standard set (docs/protocol/53 section 2.1). The client box was 0.05 wider on every side, so the client's front edge met a low step (carpet, slab, snow layer, campfire, trapdoor, the 1/16 lip where a dirt path, farmland or soul sand meets the grass) 0.05 before the core's box did. The client stepped up (rising by the step, on-ground claim), the core saw a rise while claiming the ground with no support under its own narrower box (`MOVE-SUPPORTED`, the endless-climb rule of move-check.cob) and refused the move with Player Position; the client was put back, walked again, and was refused again every tick: the shake and the blocked movement. The shape tables themselves (CTBSDATA / `BSH-COLL-ID`) are shared by both sides and agree; the lane bugbed (beds, path before a door) is a different cause and the tables were not touched.

Measured with `tests/convergence/acc_bugstep_village.cob` (headless: the client's single-player host, a superflat survival world from the generated packs; 13 village-like features set by the core in front of the Player, who walks into each with the real controls W, W + Space, Left Ctrl + W, Left Ctrl + W + Space and Left Shift + W for 130 frames; counted per run: Player Position corrections the core sent (`CN-TELEPORT-ID`), frames where z went backwards, whether the feature was crossed as vanilla allows). Before the fix: 65 cases, 43 pass and 22 fail, 510 teleports sent and 504 backward frames over the 65 runs (a run stuck at a step: 31 to 36 corrections in 130 frames; the slab, stairs, carpet, snow, trapdoor and campfire in every walking, sprinting and sneaking run, the path, farmland and soul sand lips, the low ceiling when sneaking); after: 0 teleports, 0 backward frames, 65 of 65 pass.

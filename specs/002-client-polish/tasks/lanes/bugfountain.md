## Phase bugfountain (lane bugfountain, operator report of try15: the stronghold fountain does not flow and "I can't pick up the top block with a bucket"; "water buckets don't place either, they used to"; T43500-T43699)

Operator report: (1) "the fountain in the stronghold doesn't flow", (2) "I can't pick up the top block with a bucket", (3) "water buckets don't place either, they used to". No open task covered them; related: the lake and spring features' own notes ("the fluid tick of the document is not run: the generator keeps no tick queue", feat-spring.cob, feat-geode.cob) and struct-place.cob ("fluid ticks of placeBlock (6.5) are not made").

- [x] T43500 [bugfountain] Root cause 1 (core, `WORLD-CHUNK-LOAD`): every generator writes cells only (the chunk window has no tick queue), so the water the stronghold's RoomCrossing type 1 writes (water source on a pillar of stone bricks, 36-5 section 4) and every spring, cave aquifer water, lava and structure fluid stayed a still source until a neighbouring block next changed. 36-1 section 6.5 `placeBlock` schedules a fluid tick of delay 0 for any placed fluid and 10 section 9 post-processes aquifer fluid cells; the core did neither (the deviation was written in struct-place.cob).
- [x] T43501 [bugfountain] `core/src/fluid-post.cob` (new): `CHUNK-FLUID-POST USING cache row, count out`: every water or lava cell of a chunk the generator made whose cell below or a side cell inside the chunk holds no fluid and has no collision gets a scheduled fluid tick of delay 0 (`FLUID-SCHEDULE`); at most 128 per chunk (`BT-ADD` is linear in the queue); a world whose block tables hold no water or lava (the cobolcraft set) schedules nothing. `WORLD-CHUNK-LOAD` calls it after the overflow, block-entity and entity channels were applied, for a generated chunk only. A cell beside a neighbouring chunk is taken not to take fluid; a chunk read from a region file keeps no tick (the saved ticks are not read back).
- [x] T43502 [bugfountain] Test `core/tests/test_fluid_post.cob` (cobolcraft world, then the standard tables): no fluid block, no tick; open-air water, lava at both chunk corners and water on the bottom layer get ticks of their fluid; water shut in by stone gets none; a sheet of 256 water cells over air is capped at 128.
- [x] T43503 [bugfountain] Test `core/tests/acc_bugfountain_flow.cob` (standard world, seed 12345, the chunks at and after the nearest stronghold's start chunk; skipped as a pass without CC_GENERATED_DIR): every generated fluid cell that can spread has a pending fluid tick (red before: 0 of 8); a fountain written as the generator writes it (a stone pillar, water on top, a room of air) gets its tick from the post-processing and, with a player near, the water spreads beside the pillar's top and runs down to the floor after 30 ticks.
- [x] T43510 [bugfountain] Root cause 2 (core, `BUCKET-USE`): the empty bucket's pickup made the hit cell air whatever held the water, so aiming at a waterlogged block (fence, slab, stairs, ladder, sign, chest, coral...) took the water AND destroyed the block, and kelp and seagrass (which hold water but give none to a bucket) were destroyed too; a water bucket aimed at a dry waterloggable block put a source in the cell before it instead of waterlogging it. Now: a waterlogged block stays and dries (waterlogged=false), kelp, kelp plant, seagrass and tall seagrass are not taken, the bubble column is taken, and a water bucket aimed at a dry block with the waterlogged property waterlogs it (29a section 5).

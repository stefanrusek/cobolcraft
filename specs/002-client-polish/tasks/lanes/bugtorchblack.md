## Phase bugtorchblack (lane bugtorchblack, operator report of try15: "torches with a block above them render black; break the block above and they look right"; T42000-T42199)

Operator report: a placed torch in a stone-walled space is drawn as an almost black slanted shape when a block lies above it. Related closed work: bugtorch (T30000-T30030: the core lights torch cells), bugblack (the neighbour cell of a face that lets light through).

- [x] T42000 [bugtorchblack] Where the cause is: the core is right (test `core/tests/test_light_torch_room.cob`: a torch placed through Use Item On on the wall of a sealed stone room, under the ceiling, stores block light 14 at its cell, sky 0, 13 in the cells around it, and sends the Light Update). The client's `MESH-CELL` use in `mesher.cob` took the light of every quad from the neighbour cell in the direction of the quad's face, so the quads of a torch (thin, inside its cell, not on the border of the block) that looked up into the ceiling, or toward the wall they stand on, read stone (light 0) and were drawn at the 0.1 floor (12 of the 36 vertices of a torch).
- [x] T42001 [bugtorchblack] Test `core/tests/test_light_torch_room.cob` (generated standard set, skipped as a pass without CC_GENERATED_DIR): the placement, the ceiling above, block light 14 / 13 / 13, sky 0, and the Light Update. Green before and after (regression guard for the core side).
- [x] T42002 [bugtorchblack] Test `client/tests/test_mesher_torch_light.cob` with the hand-made pack `client/tests/fixtures/mini-torch` (stone, a standing torch and a rotated wall torch model): the 36 vertices of a wall torch and of a standing torch under a stone ceiling and against stone are found, and none is drawn below 0.4 at full daylight (only the bottom quad of the standing torch, flush with the stone floor that holds no light, stays dim). Red before (12 dim vertices each), green after.
- [x] T42003 [bugtorchblack] Fix (client): 12 section 1.3 step 2 and section 1.6: the light base of a quad is the neighbour cell only when the quad is flat in its own axis and on the border of its block (and a full cube always); any other quad takes the light of its own cell. `STATE-BAKE` (models.cob) computes `BM-OWNLIGHT` per quad (CLSTATE.cpy), `MESH-CELL` is called with `DIR-NONE` (the cell itself) for such a quad of a state that is not opaque (mesher.cob); chest quads keep the old rule (models-chest.cob). This covers every model whose quads sit inside the cell: torches (also soul, redstone, copper), lanterns, campfires, candles, plants and the top of a slab; full cubes (glowstone, sea lantern, magma) are unchanged, their faces look into the cell in front of them.
- [ ] T42004 [bugtorchblack] Not done: the emission floor of 12 section 1.4 (a state whose light emission is above the stored block light raises it) has no client table (the client has no per-state emission); the core stores the emission in the cell, so the report is fixed without it. Smooth lighting (12 sections 1.3 and 1.5, corner averaging and the zero repair) is not implemented by the client's mesher either.

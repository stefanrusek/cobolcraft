## Lane 002-lane-bugdiscount (T59500-T59599)

Operator report (try17 play): "screenshot on my desktop, discounts render wrong" (villager trading screen). The screenshot is /tmp/cc-test/screenshots/2026-11-19_10.55.04.png (the Desktop holds no newer PNG): a trade row whose price differs from the base (wheat 20 for 1 emerald with special price diff -1, bread 26 with -1) showed the base count "20", the modified count twice ("1919", "2525") and no strike-through. Core and wire are right (Merchant Offers carries the base cost, special diff, multiplier and demand; VIL-SPECIAL and VIL-PRICE follow 19 section 22.4 and 40-5 section 2.4); the fault is in the client's row drawing (40-5 section 2.10 "First-cost decorations").

Status: edits and red tests written under a hold (no build, test or coverage run until the coordinator's resume); the boxes below are ticked only after they have been run.

- [ ] T59500 [US15-client] Root cause (client): `DISCOUNT-WIDGET` (client/src/screens/menus5.cob) added the strike-through as a `WK-OVERLAY` widget while `WS-TEXT`/`WS-LENGTH` still held the modified count, and `UI-RENDER` draws the text of every widget whose kind does not clear it, so the count was drawn a second time at the overlay (offset 11 px left: "1919"). The overlay was also the hurt swatch (`C8141450`, 31 % alpha) on the UI layer, drawn under the item icon, so the strike was invisible. Fix: the strike is state 4 of the first cost's slot widget (a bare slot); `UI-SLOT` (client/src/ui-render.cob) draws an opaque red 9 x 2 quad (swatch SW-BAR-0) at (x + 7, y + 12) on the layer over the icons and the count. Red first: `test_bugdiscount_row` (client; an overlay carried text, the count 19 was on two widgets, no 9 x 2 quad on the top layer); `test_screen_40_5` expects the new widget shape.

## Lane 002-lane-bugtradestop (T58300+)

Operator bug (try16): "villagers never stop moving, they are supposed to stop moving and look at you when you try to trade with them" (the villager UI itself works).

- [x] T58300 [US15-core] Root cause (core): nothing connected the open merchant window to the villager's movement. The window only records the merchant's entity id (`MN-BENCH-X`, family 29), and `STDMOB-STEP` (stdmobs.cob) ran the villager brain, the walk target and the stroll as for any other tick, so the villager kept walking, strolling and turning to its path while the player traded. Fix: a villager's (and a wandering trader's) trading player is the player whose merchant window is open on it (`VL-TRADER`, new `core/src/mob-vil-trade.cob`, a scan of the player rows, nothing stored, so closing the window, walking out of range (`MERCHANT-VALID`) or leaving ends it); while there is one `STDMOB-STEP` takes a new first branch of its walk EVALUATE: no walk, no stroll, no push (`MOB-PUSH-TICK` skipped), the yaw turned to the player (`VL-TRADE-FACE`; the viewers get it as the head yaw too, ENTITY-MOVE sends Rotate Head) and the pitch to the player's eyes (`VL-TRADE-PITCH`, sent through ENTITY-MOVE when it differs from the pitch the viewers have; level again when the trade ends). The walk target and the walk time (`EN-DEST-*`, `EN-COOLDOWN`) are not touched, so a villager mid-walk walks on where it was when the window closes (44 x2 section 10.3 TradeWithPlayerGoal: flags jump and move, start stops the navigation; LookAtTradingPlayerGoal; 44 part 3 section 3.4).
- [x] T58301 [US15-core] A villager that is already trading ignores a second player's right click (19 section 22.4 "already trading"): `MERCHANT-INTERACT` returns without a window while `VL-TRADER` finds one, so two players cannot both hold a window on one villager.
- [x] T58302 [US15-core] Red first, then green: `acc_bugtradestop_look` through the real Interact (0x1A) and Container Close (0x12) packets and CORE-TICK: a villager in the middle of a walk (20 blocks off) stands still for 40 ticks with the window open (before the fix it walked 4.5 blocks), yaw and sent (head) yaw point at Alice (formula checked independently from the final positions), pitch looks up at Alice's eyes; a Container Close lets it walk on and levels the pitch; two players: Bobby's right click opens nothing, the villager stays for Alice, then faces Bobby when Bobby trades; Bobby walking 10 blocks away closes his window and the villager walks on; Alice leaving with the window open frees the villager; a wandering trader with a window open stays.
- [ ] T58303 [US15-core] Open, not changed here: the document's LookAndFollowTradingPlayerSink (44 part 3 section 3.4) also walks the villager toward its trading player (walk target = the player, close enough 2) while it is within 4 blocks, alive, not in water and not hurt recently; here the villager stands where it is (the operator's report asks for standing still and looking). A villager hurt while trading (the `wasHurtRecently` and in-water conditions of TradeWithPlayerGoal) keeps standing. Other mobs pushing the villager (their own `MOB-PUSH-TICK`) still add velocity to it.

## Lane 002-lane-p46d (T26500+)

Scope: T0697, retiring the stand-in `core/src/light.cob` in favour of the engine of 32 (`core/src/light-32*.cob`). Lane p46c found the engine fixed to a 4 x 4 chunk window, 4 block sections and 64 states; this lane removes those three limits and builds the bridge to the chunk cache, and stops where the remaining work is a decision and a performance problem, not documentation.

- [x] T26500 Engine geometry (`core/copy/L32.cpy`, `core/src/light-32.cob` to `light-32e.cob`): the window is a sliding 4 x 4 torus (widened to 6 x 6 by T26503) (chunk (x, z) lives in slot (z + 1 mod 4) * 4 + (x + 1 mod 4) + 1 and owns it while `EN-OWN-X` / `EN-OWN-Z` name it; the default window, chunks -1 to 2, keeps the old numbering), the column height is `EN-NS` block sections from section `EN-MINSEC` (default 4 from 0, at most 24: 16 and 24 sections from Y -64 work), the state table is 40002 entries. New programs `L32-SET-DIMENSION` and `L32-CLAIM-CHUNK` (a slot taken by another chunk is cleared). The 50000-pair work queues compact when full (`L32-PUSH-INC`, `L32-PUSH-DEC`) instead of overflowing. Test `core/tests/test_light_32_window.cob` (6 cases; red: link failure, `L32-SET-DIMENSION` and `L32-CLAIM-CHUNK` undefined): a chunk at (100, -50), a slot taken by chunk (3, 0), 16 sections (emitter at Y 200), 24 sections from Y -64 (emitter at Y -60, sky 15 at Y 319), states 30000 and 39999, sky light under an overhang (15, 14, 9, 5, 5, 14, 15 along the roof; the stand-in gave 0 there). The nine `test_light_32_*` programs and `test_light_covb` pass unchanged.
- [x] T26501 Engine states from the registry (`core/src/light-32f.cob`): `L32-LOAD-REGISTRY` defines every block state of the open content set as engine state id + 2 (emission `CB-S-LIGHT`; canOcclude `CB-OPAQUE`; occlusion shape the outline `BSH-OUT`, solidRender when a box is the full cube; fluid not empty for waterlogged states, `LiquidBlock` and the always-wet plants; propagatesSkylightDown by the 24 part 1 section 5.2 default; dampening and useShape from `L32-BLOCK-RULES` (32 section 7); the six face masks from the shape boxes, `L32-FACE-MASKS`), `L32-ENGINE-STATE` (air is 0), `L32-DIM-RESET` (a dimension's engine keeps the loaded states). Test `core/tests/test_light_32_registry.cob` on the standard set (13 cases: stone, glass, leaves, cobweb, torch, glowstone, bottom, top, waterlogged and double slabs, water, air, reset). Red by construction (the programs did not exist); two defects found by the test and fixed where they were: the property list text is `name[values]`, and the builder gives equal shapes more than one id so the full cube is read from the boxes.
- [x] T26502 Cache bridge (`core/src/light-32g.cob`): `L32-LOAD-CHUNK` (cache cells to a window slot), `L32-STORE-LIGHT` (published layers to the cache's light arrays), `L32-LIGHT-ROW` (the chunk with the loaded chunks of its 3 x 3, one engine run). Test `core/tests/test_light_32_cache.cob` (4 cases, cobolcraft chunk (0, 0)): the engine's block light equals the stand-in's in every byte (sum 112103) and its sky light differs in one byte, the lucite cell (8, 58, 8): engine 14, stand-in 0.
- [x] T26503 Persistent sliding window (`core/src/light-32g.cob`, `core/copy/L32WIN.cpy`, `core/copy/L32.cpy`): the engine now keeps a 6 x 6 window of chunk columns between calls (the 5 x 5 chunks around a chunk, so that the light of the chunk and of its 8 neighbours is exact; `EN-SLOTMAP` keeps the slot numbers 1 to 16 of the first window for chunks -1 to 2), a chunk is loaded into a slot once and compared with the cache (a memcmp of its bytes) on every later call: cells that differ go through `L32-SET-BLOCK` (`L32-SLOT-SYNC`, up to 2000, else the window is rebuilt), `L32-BLOCK-CHANGED` patches the one changed cell, `L32-LIGHT-ROW` makes the window current, stores the light of the chunk and its loaded neighbours (`L32-STORE-LIGHT`) and sets `CK-LIT`. Sped up: sky sections above every lowest source are filled at once (`L32-SKY-FILL-OPEN`; `L32-CHUNK-START-SKY` was 1.86 s of the 1.96 s of the first measurement, nine chunks), `L32-SET-STORED` flags only the sections a border cell touches, `L32-CHUNK-START-BLOCK` skips air sections, a block section that is all state 0 is not converted, sections without a layer are stored as a row copy, not cell by cell. Test `core/tests/test_light_32_bridge.cob` (9 cases, cobolcraft world): the light the window keeps incrementally (a chunk lit after its neighbour, block changes, a source on a chunk border, cells changed behind its back, 2304 changed cells, a dimension without sky, a new content epoch) equals the light a rebuild of the window gives, byte for byte in the cache; `test_light_32_window` case 2 moved to the 6-column slots.
- [x] T26504 Integer arithmetic in the engine's hot paths (`core/src/light-32.cob`, `light-32c.cob`, `core/copy/L32.cpy`): `L32-LOCATE` takes the local coordinate of each axis from the low byte of the coordinate (`EN-TL16`: the low four bits of a binary integer are its value mod 16, also when negative), keeps the last chunk column found (`EN-M-*`, cleared when a slot changes owner) and takes the section from `EN-SYTAB`, so a cell in the chunk last found needs no `FUNCTION MOD` and no division; `L32-STATE-AT` reads its block from the section id; `L32-PROP-INC` and `L32-PROP-DEC` read a direction's bit from a table. A `sample` profile of one standard chunk step before: `L32-LOCATE` 60 percent of the samples, after: 33 percent. Measured user CPU of the five chunks a window step adds, standard world, `clock()`, serial: 0.90 to 1.13 s before, 0.42 to 0.49 s after (84 to 98 ms a chunk against the stand-in's 51 to 53 ms, 1.6 to 1.8 times). The nine `test_light_32_*`, `test_light_covb`, window, registry, cache and bridge tests pass unchanged (118), `cover-changed` shows 0 uncovered changed lines, lint is clean.
- [ ] T26504b T0697 NOT DONE, the engine stays unwired and `light.cob` stays. Measured (user CPU, `clock()`, one process, serial, `-debug` build; load average 14 to 48), 64 chunks lit after all are loaded, row by row, stand-in against engine: cobolcraft 2.6 s (40 ms a chunk) against 9.3 s (145 ms a chunk); standard 3.3 s (51 ms a chunk) against 49.8 s (779 ms a chunk). A block change in a chunk of the window: 1.4 ms (cobolcraft) and 2.0 ms (standard) against 0.16 ms; the first block change outside the window (the window of 5 x 5 chunks is rebuilt from the blocks and relit from scratch): 0.66 s (cobolcraft) and 2.5 s (standard), against 11 ms for the stand-in. So a sweep that keeps moving one chunk at a time costs 1.6 to 1.8 times the stand-in (target met), but every jump of the window (a row restart in the sweep, a block change far from the last one, a second player) costs a rebuild of 25 chunks, which is where the 15 times of the 64-chunk standard run and the 230 times of the out-of-window block change come from. Not done: (a) loading stored light (32 section 8.4): queue the cache's light arrays as section data and light the chunk with `lighted` 1. The LightData of a chunk reports every light section (all-zero as empty), so the cache's arrays are the same data a client applies and the missing-layer ambiguity does not arise for a chunk the engine or the packets wrote; sections of air above the surface stay implicit because the engine drops queued data for sections that store no light. With the stored light a window of 3 x 3 chunks is exact for a block change or a new chunk (a light wave reaches at most 15 cells, so it does not reach the outer border of the ring), and a move costs the copy of blocks and light of up to 9 chunks (about 10 to 15 ms of block conversion for each standard chunk, so 100 ms or more, not a few); (b) a few milliseconds for an isolated far block change needs the blocks to be loaded lazily by section and cell neighbourhood, which the engine's flat block table does not do. Neither was started.
- [ ] T26504c Stored light (32 section 8.4) tried and put back, finding for whoever continues: queueing the cache's light arrays (`retainData`, `queueSectionData` per light section, the chunk lit with `lighted` 1) and a 3 x 3 window works for a chunk whose blocks are the ones its light was computed from, but it is unsound in two cases the cache cannot tell the engine about: (a) a chunk loaded into the window with a block that changed while it was outside it (the stored light assumed the old block, so a removed roof or a removed source is never undone; a placed source is only half right): the fix is to pass the old state of the changed cell (`WORLD-SET-BLOCK` has it as `WS-OLD`, the `LIGHT-BATCH-NOTE` rows would carry it) and to load the chunk with the old state in that cell, then apply the new one through `L32-SET-BLOCK`; (b) cells written into a lit chunk without notice (the overflow of `gen-overflow.cob`, `CK-RESEND`) and a window rebuilt for a chunk that differs in more than 2000 cells: the neighbours' stored light is stale, so those chunks must be lit from their blocks (never-lit, `CK-LIT` 0). The test that showed it is the one that rebuilds the window after 2304 changed cells: with stored light the neighbour read differently from a rebuild from the blocks. Lazy conversion of block sections (convert only the sections within 15 cells of a change) was not started; it also needs the column's sky sources from the whole column of blocks, which a per-section table does not give.

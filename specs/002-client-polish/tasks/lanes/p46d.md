## Lane 002-lane-p46d (T26500+)

Scope: T0697, retiring the stand-in `core/src/light.cob` in favour of the engine of 32 (`core/src/light-32*.cob`). Lane p46c found the engine fixed to a 4 x 4 chunk window, 4 block sections and 64 states; this lane removes those three limits and builds the bridge to the chunk cache, and stops where the remaining work is a decision and a performance problem, not documentation.

- [x] T26500 Engine geometry (`core/copy/L32.cpy`, `core/src/light-32.cob` to `light-32e.cob`): the window is a sliding 4 x 4 torus (chunk (x, z) lives in slot (z + 1 mod 4) * 4 + (x + 1 mod 4) + 1 and owns it while `EN-OWN-X` / `EN-OWN-Z` name it; the default window, chunks -1 to 2, keeps the old numbering), the column height is `EN-NS` block sections from section `EN-MINSEC` (default 4 from 0, at most 24: 16 and 24 sections from Y -64 work), the state table is 40002 entries. New programs `L32-SET-DIMENSION` and `L32-CLAIM-CHUNK` (a slot taken by another chunk is cleared). The 50000-pair work queues compact when full (`L32-PUSH-INC`, `L32-PUSH-DEC`) instead of overflowing. Test `core/tests/test_light_32_window.cob` (6 cases; red: link failure, `L32-SET-DIMENSION` and `L32-CLAIM-CHUNK` undefined): a chunk at (100, -50), a slot taken by chunk (3, 0), 16 sections (emitter at Y 200), 24 sections from Y -64 (emitter at Y -60, sky 15 at Y 319), states 30000 and 39999, sky light under an overhang (15, 14, 9, 5, 5, 14, 15 along the roof; the stand-in gave 0 there). The nine `test_light_32_*` programs and `test_light_covb` pass unchanged.
- [x] T26501 Engine states from the registry (`core/src/light-32f.cob`): `L32-LOAD-REGISTRY` defines every block state of the open content set as engine state id + 2 (emission `CB-S-LIGHT`; canOcclude `CB-OPAQUE`; occlusion shape the outline `BSH-OUT`, solidRender when a box is the full cube; fluid not empty for waterlogged states, `LiquidBlock` and the always-wet plants; propagatesSkylightDown by the 24 part 1 section 5.2 default; dampening and useShape from `L32-BLOCK-RULES` (32 section 7); the six face masks from the shape boxes, `L32-FACE-MASKS`), `L32-ENGINE-STATE` (air is 0), `L32-DIM-RESET` (a dimension's engine keeps the loaded states). Test `core/tests/test_light_32_registry.cob` on the standard set (13 cases: stone, glass, leaves, cobweb, torch, glowstone, bottom, top, waterlogged and double slabs, water, air, reset). Red by construction (the programs did not exist); two defects found by the test and fixed where they were: the property list text is `name[values]`, and the builder gives equal shapes more than one id so the full cube is read from the boxes.
- [x] T26502 Cache bridge (`core/src/light-32g.cob`): `L32-LOAD-CHUNK` (cache cells to a window slot), `L32-STORE-LIGHT` (published layers to the cache's light arrays), `L32-LIGHT-ROW` (the chunk with the loaded chunks of its 3 x 3, one engine run). Test `core/tests/test_light_32_cache.cob` (4 cases, cobolcraft chunk (0, 0)): the engine's block light equals the stand-in's in every byte (sum 112103) and its sky light differs in one byte, the lucite cell (8, 58, 8): engine 14, stand-in 0.
- [ ] T26503 T0697 NOT DONE, `light.cob` stays and no caller was switched. What remains is not documentation (32, 24 and 51 give every rule used above) but two decisions and a cost: (1) cost: one `L32-LIGHT-ROW` (load nine chunks, source scan, run, store) measures about 1.5 s of CPU for one chunk of the cobolcraft world, where the stand-in takes milliseconds; worldgen lights every chunk it makes and block updates relight on every change, so the engine needs a persistent window (reload only chunks whose cells changed, which needs a change counter on every writer of the chunk cache) and a faster block-source scan and chunk copy before any caller can move; (2) semantics: the stand-in is dark at and below a column's top block (lucite included; asserted by `test_light` and `test_light_extra`) and the engine spreads sky light sideways under overhangs and through lucite, so the stand-in tests that assert it change with the switch, and so would any wire fixture that carries sky light beside a ledge or a lucite block; (3) the stand-in's per-chunk `CK-LIT` ordering and `WORLD-LIGHT-READY` (chunk-feed.cob) assume a chunk is lit on its own, the engine lights a neighbourhood.
- [ ] T26504 Known gap found on the way, in `content/src/block-shapes.cob` (not this lane's): the outline of `cobweb` is a full cube in doc 51 row R-A2 but the table holds a second full-cube id for it that is not shape id 1 (fixed here by reading the boxes), and docs 51 lists cauldrons, hoppers, bells, scaffolding, pistons, composters and grindstones as not yet covered (default full cube), so their dampening and face masks in the engine follow the default until those shapes exist.

Decisions where the document leaves the choice (30-8 section 15)
- Open question 17 (packet handler against the self-simulation pass): a player's on-ground flag is the packet's own flag and no extra pass with m = 0 is run; the core never replays a packet through collisions, so m is the accepted displacement. A displacement over 8 blocks is a teleport (commands, a pet's jump, respawn): no counter changes, nothing is posted.
- Open question 7 (bounce reflection): only the event is built. A landing at more than the gravity 0.08 in one pass on a slime block, a bed or a shelf mushroom, without the shift key and not on a suppresses_bounce block, posts bounce after hit_ground; the reflected speed is not computed.
- The supporting block is the cell holding (x, y - 1e-5, z), the cell below it when that is air and the entity is on the ground (fences and walls), whose legacy cell is the same; the supporting-block search of 14.1.3 itself is not built.
- The vibration owner of an arrow is its EN-TARGET-ID (p49w); a bat is never resting (the core has no resting bat), so every bat flaps; a phantom's flap offset is its entity id mod 25.

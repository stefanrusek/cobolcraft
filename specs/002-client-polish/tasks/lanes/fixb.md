## Lane 002-lane-fixb (T6600+)

Operator play of try6, client rendering (release build).

- [x] T6600 Leaves, trapdoors, doors (and panes, plants, every cut-out sprite) looked wrong, and ice often did too: `ATLAS-BUILD` marked a sprite translucent when any pixel had alpha below 255, so every sprite with clear pixels (alpha 0) went to the translucent mesh, which is drawn without depth writes and unsorted inside a section: the faces of leaves, doors and trapdoors were blended over each other in cell order (and over the ice in the same layer). 08 section 5.3 (`SpriteContents.computeTransparency`) has three classes: opaque, cut out (alpha 0 pixels only) and translucent (some alpha neither 0 nor 255). `ATLAS-TRANSLUCENT` (client/src/atlas.cob) now sets `AT-TRANSLUCENT` for the partial alpha class only; cut-out faces go to the opaque mesh, where the world shader drops texels under alpha 0.1 and the depth buffer orders the faces. Ice, frosted ice, water and stained glass (partial alpha) stay translucent; packed and blue ice are opaque sprites. Test `client/tests/test_atlas_layer.cob` (red: link failure of `ATLAS-TRANSLUCENT`, `scratchpad/fixb/red-layer.txt`).
- [x] T6601 Dropped items of a standard world were not drawn: `GROUND-ITEMS-MESH` took the rows of type `ET-LOOSE-ITEM` (1, the stand-in loose_item), but the entity of the standard set is `item` (id 72) and its type 1 is acacia_chest_boat. It now takes a row with `EN-VANILLA` 0 and type 1, or `EN-VANILLA` 1 and key `item`. Test `client/tests/test_ground_item_standard.cob` (red: `scratchpad/fixb/red-ground-standard.txt`). B3 (item drawn twice, one copy rotated) could not be reproduced by reading the hand (client/src/hand.cob: one cube of 6 quads or one extruded icon), the icon (`UI-SLOT`: one quad) and the ground-item path (one quad per row); needs the operator's screenshot or the item and model names. Not done: the 3D dropped item of 34 part 9 section 9.2 (the quad is the stand-in appearance), spider eyes (T6602 notes).

## Phase bugsmooth (lane bugsmooth, play-test bug of try18: "despite 60 fps there is a little bit of jumpiness while moving around"; T60700-T60799)

Root cause (client, not core or shim): the player (physics.cob PHYS-TICK), every replica entity and the field of view change once per 50 ms client tick, and the frame drew the state of the last tick (camera.cob CAMERA-PLACE took PL-X, PL-Y + eye height; the entity meshes took EN-X; render-list.cob took PL-FOV-MOD), so at 60 fps the world stood still for two frames of three and then jumped by a whole tick's walk. Vanilla draws lerp(partialTick, old, current) of all of them (53 section 7 the camera eye, 12 section 7.2 the field of view, 34-1 section 4 entity position and rotation). A second source: CL-CLOCK counted hundredths (CURRENT-DATE), so a frame time was quantised to 10 ms and an interpolated partial tick would have jittered by up to 0.2.

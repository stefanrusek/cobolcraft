## Lane 002-lane-bughang (T46200+)

Operator report from the try15 play: "the game has hung trying to open a world" (client pid 77241 sampled during the hang: main thread busy in COBOL decimal arithmetic, 97% CPU). The operator's disk was full at the time, so a failing write was suspected. Base of the lane: 002-try15 6789c8b5.

- [x] T46200 [US16] (lane bughang) Root cause found, in the core (not the client, not the shim, not the disk): the sampled stack (symbolised from the try15 release binary) is PLAY-JOIN, PLAY-ARRIVE, CHUNKS-INITIAL-BATCH, CHUNK-PACKET-BUILD, WORLD-CHUNK, LIGHT-CHUNK, WORLD-CHUNK-PEEK, WORLD-CHUNK-LOAD, WORLDGEN-DIM-CHUNK, WORLDGEN-STANDARD-CHUNK, GEN-STANDARD-INSTALL, GEN-JOB-RUN-ALL, GEN-STAGE-SURFACE: the whole standard terrain pipeline, chunk after chunk, inside the join. `WORLDGEN-STANDARD-CHUNK` and `WORLD-IS-DEBUG` chose the generator from `CS-WORLD-TYPE` of the settings the world was opened with, while a world opened from the list takes its type from its level.dat (`WORLD-TYPE-DECIDE` into `CW-WORLD-TYPE`) and the client always asks for `cobolcraft` there (`SP-CREATE-TYPE` keeps its default without the create screen, `sp-host.cob`). A saved superflat world therefore got standard terrain for every chunk (`WORLD-GEN-OWN`, which reads `CW-WORLD-TYPE`, said "own generator": no chunk feed, no generation queue), and a saved debug world lost its grid. The newest save `New World (7)` is a superflat world: reproduced from a copy of it with the settings the client passes (request cobolcraft, view distance 12): the same stack, still running after more than 2 minutes of CPU.
- [x] T46201 [US16] (lane bughang) Red first: `core/tests/test_reopen_world_type.cob` (real data, CC_GENERATED_DIR; skipped as a pass without it): a superflat world saved and reopened with the request cobolcraft joins in under 20 s, its chunk is flat (grass at -61, air at -60), and a debug world reopened that way is still `WORLD-IS-DEBUG`. Failed before the fix: the join time, the flat chunk and the debug case (3 of 6 cases).
- [x] T46202 [US16] (lane bughang) Fix: `core/src/worldgen-standard.cob` WORLDGEN-STANDARD-CHUNK and WORLD-IS-DEBUG read `CW-WORLD-TYPE` (the decided type), never the request. `core/tests/test_debug_no_terrain.cob` and `test_debug_no_ticks.cob` faked the type by editing `CW-SETTINGS` only: they set `CW-WORLD-TYPE` too.
- [x] T46203 [US16] (lane bughang) Second cause of the same freeze, measured after T46202 from the copy of `New World (7)`: a superflat join still made its whole first batch inside the join (`CHUNK-FEED-ON` was 0 for it): view distance 12 took 113 s, view 6 (169 chunks) 7.3 s (a flat chunk with its light, LIGHT-FRESH, costs 40 to 180 ms). `core/src/chunk-feed.cob` CHUNK-FEED-ON now answers 1 for the overworld of a superflat world: the chunks come from the generation queue over the ticks (the way the Nether and the standard overworld already do, T6301).

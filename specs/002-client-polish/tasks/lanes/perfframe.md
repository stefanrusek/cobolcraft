## Lane 002-lane-perfframe (T41000+)

Operator report: the game is judged by frames per second (21 to 34 at view 12; try15 "noticeably smoother"). Base: 002-lane-bugmesh d0a869a5. Goal: less client CPU per frame, no rendered output changed. Every optimisation below has an equivalence test (a digest taken from the code before the change, byte for byte) and a measured before/after.

- [x] T41000 [US3-client] (lane perfframe) Frame benchmark `tests/convergence/acc_perfframe_bench.cob`: a standard single-player world (fixed seed, the in-process core, real generated chunks) stepped until every chunk has arrived and is meshed, rules set so the blocks stay as generated, every section meshed again (timed, digested), then PF_MOBS mobs summoned in a ring (zombie, creeper, skeleton, spider, cow, sheep, pig, chicken) and PF_FRAMES frames measured in user CPU (C clock()): the whole of CL-STEP with the core tick (CV-STEP at PF_FRAME_MS, default 25: a tick every other frame, a game at 40 frames a second), and run again on the same state per phase RENDER-LIST-BUILD, ENTITIES-MESH, MESH-DIRTY idle, SCREEN-BUILD with UI-RENDER. PF_VIEW (3), PF_MOBS (24), PF_SEED, PF_DIR, PF_MESH_PASSES, PF_TRACE. Profiled with `sample` on the running binary (scratchpad/perfframe/*.sample, byprog.py attributes the samples to COBOL programs including the libcob and GMP calls they cause). The submit (SUBMIT-FRAME) needs a window and is not measured. Two findings of the profile outside the lane's scope: the generated worlds differ between two runs (features across chunk borders depend on the arrival order from the generation workers), so a digest of real chunks is not repeatable; and the digests below use synthetic replicas.
- [x] T41001 [US3-client] (lane perfframe) `client/tests/test_entities_digest.cob`: ENTITIES-MESH of every model of the table with an entity type (116 rows) in four states (rest; walk, head turn, pitch; aggressive, baby, swinging; the eye elsewhere), CRC-32 of the vertices summed over batches of 16: 6106902634934864984, identical before and after every change.
- [x] T41002 [US3-client] (lane perfframe) `client/tests/test_render_list_digest.cob`: RENDER-LIST-BUILD over a 625-row replica (view 12) from 60 eyes (position, yaw, pitch), digest of every draw record (slot, pipeline, texture, 16 matrix floats), of the upload slots and counts, of the projection, view and fog: identical before and after; 60 frames bounded at 250 ms of CPU.
- [x] T41003 [US3-client] (lane perfframe) ANIM-TICK (atlas.cob): the per-mille blend of an interpolated sprite is table reads (a x keep, b x share, the quotient of the sum by 1000: 256 000 entries made on the first blend) in place of a decimal COMPUTE a byte. `client/tests/test_anim_blend.cob` (every byte of 8 sprites of 32 x 32 over two cycles against the formula; red before: 552 ms for 200 ticks against the 150 ms bound; 14.6 ms after, 38 times).
- [x] T41004 [US3-client] (lane perfframe) RENDER-LIST-BUILD and SECTION-MATRIX (render-list.cob): the frustum planes as coefficients and magnitudes once a frame (FUNCTION ABS was 6 percent of a frame at view 12), the squared distance of translucent sections as products, the chunk centre and corner as floats kept per row, the section offsets once a frame and a row, the projection x view as FLOAT-SHORT once a frame, the tangent of the field of view kept while it is the same (exact group compare), float compares against a float zero (a float compared with an integer literal is 230 ns through libcob, 4 ns against a float field). The digest of T41002 is unchanged; 6.7 ms to 1.9 ms a frame at view 12 (400 ms to 112 ms for the 60 frames of the test).
- [x] T41005 [US3-client] (lane perfframe) ENTITY-MODEL-EMIT and ENTITIES-MESH (entities-models.cob, entities-render.cob): the face corner order and the texel picks of EF7-FACE-VERTS and EF7-VERTEX-UV are binary tables made by calling them once, the op codes of the render sequence binary, the copy of the baked vertices keeps the vertex count in a field of the program (a client-record field as a subscript costs a call each time, eight a vertex) and converts the tint once, float compares against float fields (the entity cull, the distance cap, a part's rotation). A bake costs 1.29 ms before, 0.86 ms after (116 models, 860 bakes: 1109 ms to 735 ms); the cached frame of 70 mobs (26 172 vertices) costs 2.3 ms of CPU before, 1.1 ms after (existing `test_entities_bench`, now bounded on CPU time: its wall-clock reading at a machine load of 100 was 10.6 to 12.3 ms before, 2.5 to 8 ms after, 5 times the CPU time). Digest of T41001 unchanged.
- [x] T41006 [US3-client] (lane perfframe) MESH-CELL and MESH-HIDDEN (mesher.cob): the index arithmetic of a neighbour cell (y x 256 + z x 16 + x, the light byte and its nibble: a COMPUTE and DIVIDEs at 100 ns each) is table reads and ADDs; MESH-SECTION's row-state and y by ADD. `client/tests/test_mesher_digest.cob` (9 chunks of hashed air, greystone, lucite and water, 45 sections, CRC-32 of both meshes: 8248849812848199119 before and after): 946 ms to 630 ms (a third less).
- [x] T41007 [US3-client, core] (lane perfframe) The in-process core's mob pathing read cells through WORLD-GET-BLOCK 27 percent of a frame at view 3 (WORLD-FIND and WORLD-GET-BLOCK, the MOD and DIVISION chain of the chunk lookup): WORLD-GET-BLOCK keeps the last chunk (position box, row, dimension) and reads the cell at once while the row is in use and holds that chunk; WORLD-FIND keeps its last row the same way; WORLD-GET-BLOCK and WORLD-PEEK-BLOCK floor-divide by DIVIDE with a remainder. `core/tests/test_world_find_memo.cob` (same row, another chunk in between, the world reopened frees the memo's row, get and peek agree across borders and west of x 0; 20 000 reads of one chunk: 29.4 ms before, 1.3 ms after, 23 times).

Measured (release build -O2, in-process core, view 12, 40 mobs, 400 frames, CV-STEP at 25 ms, user CPU of the process, machine load average 55 to 75, two runs side by side, each its own generated world, so the entity counts differ: 48 rows and 3 816 vertices a frame before, 217 rows and 4 997 after): mean frame of CL-STEP with the core tick 22.1 ms before, 18.4 ms after (longest frame 43.7 and 37.1 ms); RENDER-LIST-BUILD run again on the same state 11.7 ms before, 8.0 ms after (night: the sky meshes are in it; the 625-row replica test without sky: 6.7 ms to 1.9 ms); all 15 000 sections meshed again 43.4 s before, 32.9 s after (5 983 with vertices: 7.2 ms to 5.5 ms a section). At view 3 before the lane the profile of a frame (release, sample) was 47 percent the core tick (mob pathing in WORLD-FIND and WORLD-GET-BLOCK 27 percent, MOB-YAW-TOWARD 6), ENTITY-MODEL-EMIT and its EF7 programs 19, RENDER-LIST-BUILD with SECTION-MATRIX 17, ANIM-TICK 10; at view 6 after it is ENTITY-MODEL-EMIT with the anim programs 22, SECTION-MATRIX 13 (the benchmark runs the render list twice a frame), VANILLA-ITEMS-MESH 11 and GI-IN-REACH 7.5 (dropped items in the scene), RENDER-LIST-BUILD 10; the core tick is out of the first ten. `test_entities_bench` (70 mobs, wall clock, load average 100): 10.6 to 12.3 ms before, 2.5 to 8 ms after; CPU time 2.3 ms before, 1.1 ms after, its bounds are now on CPU time.

Not done, with the measured reason: MOB-YAW-TOWARD takes ATAN through libcob (6 percent at view 3): libm's atan differs from libcob's multi-precision one in the last bit, which a FLOAT-SHORT yaw would show now and then, so it is not changed (the shim does not take over cob_intr_atan for that reason); the float-to-integer conversion (COMPUTE binary = float, 250 ns) and FUNCTION ABS and INTEGER of a float (420 ns) remain all over the entity animation programs (EMD-ANIM, EM3-QUAD-ANIM); a section opacity bitmap for MESH-HIDDEN, and caching the matrix columns of SECTION-MATRIX per row; VANILLA-ITEMS-MESH (sin, cos and a LCG per dropped item each frame); the submit (needs a window). Atlas rebuilds (ENTITY-ATLAS-BUILD) when a new model enters the replica cost 10 ms and more of MISSING-TEXTURE (a decimal MOD a pixel) when a pack lacks the entity texture.

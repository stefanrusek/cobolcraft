## Lane 002-lane-bugstall (T58000+)

Operator report from the try16 play (release build): "framerate is 0 for the first minute or so after loading a world or respawning". Base of the lane: origin/002-client-polish db901a9a. Cause: the core, in the 50 ms tick, generated whole standard chunks on the main thread (4 to 5 s each), through a fluid write at the border of the loaded area (core, not client or shim).

Reports the longest frame, the frames over 100 ms, the frame at which every chunk of the view is in the replica and the frame at which nothing is left to mesh. - [x] T58001 [US3-client] (lane bugstall) A fluid tick of a cell at the edge of a loaded chunk does not make the neighbouring chunk in the tick. The unloaded neighbour read as air while the generation workers make it (WORLD-GET-BLOCK defers: void), the water spread into it, and WORLD-SET-BLOCK generated the whole chunk in the tick, once per chunk at the border of the loaded area, for as long as the world streamed in. `FLUID-EDGE-DEFER` (core/src/fluids-world-29a.cob): 1 when a chunk beside the cell is not loaded and the workers are to make it (WORLD-GET-DEFER, which queues it); FLUID-TICK then puts the cell's own tick back 10 ticks (FLUID-SCHEDULE) and returns; a cell not at an edge and a world without workers behave as before. Red test, failing before (2 cases): `core/tests/test_fluid_defer.cob` (edge water does not load the neighbour, the chunk is queued, the tick is put back; away from the edge it spreads; with the neighbour loaded it spreads into it; without workers the chunk is made where the write stands).

+## Phase bugleafdecay (lane bugleafdecay, operator report of try18: "leaves break over time after their logs are gone"; T61800-T61899)
+
+Operator report: leaves must decay and drop saplings, sticks or apples after the logs that support them are removed. Vanilla flow (docs/protocol/29b part 3 section 5, 29 sections 3 and 4, 33 section 2.4): a leaves block has `distance` 1 to 7, `persistent` and `waterlogged`; its distance is the distance to the nearest log through leaves (0 at a log, a neighbour's distance plus 1, 7 at most); a shape update schedules a tick after 1 game tick when the neighbour that sent it says the distance is not the block's; the scheduled tick recomputes the distance with neighbour updates (one block per game tick); a leaf is randomly ticking at distance 7 unless persistent and then drops its loot table (no tool) and is removed; a leaves item places `persistent = true`.

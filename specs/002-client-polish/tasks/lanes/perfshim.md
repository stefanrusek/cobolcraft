## Lane 002-lane-perfshim (T59300+)

Operator report: really bad performance in the release build. Lane perfframe found that libcob's multi precision paths dominate the hot loops of the client frame and the core tick. Base: origin/002-client-polish db901a9a. Goal: take the libcob calls that cost most over in `shim/src/cc_fpfast.c` (the FPFAST overlay, `tools/package.mk` name lists only), with libcob's own result for every input class the project uses, and the FPFAST contract unchanged where it already accepts a deviation (float arithmetic in IEEE double; SIN and COS in libm's double). No compiler flag and no other makefile line changed.

Profile of the release build (macOS `sample`, `tests/convergence/acc_perfframe_bench` at view 6, 40 mobs, before the lane; share of the samples a call was on the stack): `cob_decimal_get_field` 13.7 percent (a float stored into a binary field, GI-IN-REACH, BT-HEAD, VANILLA-ITEMS-MESH), `cob_mpf_sin` and `cob_mpf_cos` 19 (EMD-ANIM, EM3-QUAD-ANIM, EMD-A-BOB: `FUNCTION COS(x * FUNCTION PI / 180)` fell to libcob because FUNCTION PI is a 96 digit decimal), `cob_intr_integer` 5.7 (ENTITY-BOX-HITS, WORLD-CELL: `FUNCTION INTEGER(x / 16)`), `cob_intr_binop` 5.4 (the integer quotient of the same idiom), `cob_move` 5.4 (a literal moved into a float), `cob_intr_tan` 4 (HAND-PROJECT), `cob_get_int` and `cob_get_llint` 6, `cob_cmp_llint` and `cob_cmp_float` 2, and the shim's own helpers (tag, untag, as_double: 19 percent of the samples, because the shim C is compiled without optimisation, see T59310).

- [x] T59300 [US3-client] (lane perfshim) Micro benchmark of every construct before and after (`scratchpad` C-free COBOL program generated by a script, 1 000 000 iterations each, user CPU, ns per operation with the loop subtracted): a float compared with an integer literal 290 ns to 8 (1.5 with the shim at -O2), a float stored into a binary field 356 ns to 18 (6), `MOVE F TO B` 351 to 16, `COMPUTE B = F * 16` 369 to 43 (8), `MOVE 0.5 TO F` 119 to 16 (3), `FUNCTION INTEGER(F)` 403 to 35 (21), `FUNCTION ABS(F)` 689 to 28 (6), `FUNCTION ATAN(F)` 264 000 to 23, `FUNCTION COS(F * FUNCTION PI / 180)` 110 000 to 660 (the call of FUNCTION PI itself was 500 ns of that, kept now), `FUNCTION INTEGER(B / 16)` 615 to under 100, `FUNCTION MAX(B / 16, 0)` 603. Measured with the machine at a load average of 40 to 55.
- [x] T59301 [US3-client, core] (lane perfshim) `ccfp_cmp_llint` (`cob_cmp_llint`): a float against an integer is the sign of the exact difference, with libcob's answer for a zero (-n clamped to an int); a negative zero, a non-finite float, an integer above 2^53 and any other field are libcob's. `ccfp_cmp` and `ccfp_numeric_cmp` (`cob_cmp`, `cob_numeric_cmp`): a float against a float, an integer below 2^53 or a DISPLAY number of up to 15 digits and 0 to 22 decimals (a literal, a PIC 9 item, a signed one with a leading separate sign) gives `cob_cmp_float`'s answer, with its relative 1e-7 tolerance and libcob's own conversion of the DISPLAY number (its double is cut toward zero, not rounded). Tests: `test_cc_fpfast.c` `compare_with_integer`, `compare_fields` (specials, nextafter neighbours of literals, packed, scaled and malformed fields, 20 000 random floats).
- [x] T59302 [US3-client, core] (lane perfshim) `ccfp_decimal_get_field`: a tagged float stored into a binary integer field (no decimals, no ROUNDED, the integer part within the field's digits and bytes) is stored as libcob stores the decimal of it (cut toward zero); an untagged decimal of up to 53 bits with 0 to 22 decimals (a literal, an integer, their product) stored into a float field is libcob's cut double (`cut_quotient`: C's quotient and the sign of the fused residual). Everything else (ROUNDED, a size error, a wider number, a field with decimals, a DISPLAY target) is libcob's. A negative zero is stored as zero (libcob's own store of it reads an unset decimal). Tests: `float_into_integer` (10 field kinds, 7 option sets, specials and random: bytes and status equal to libcob's), `decimal_into_float_exact` (30 000 random literals, integers and products).
- [x] T59303 [US3-client, core] (lane perfshim) `ccfp_move` (`cob_move`): a DISPLAY number or binary integer into a float, a float into a binary integer, with `cob_move`'s own result (bytes), anything else `cob_move`. `ccfp_get_int` and `ccfp_get_llint`: a binary field of scale 0. Tests: `move_numbers` (13 target kinds, DISPLAY literals unsigned and signed at random, binary integers, floats), `get_integers`.
- [x] T59304 [US3-client, core] (lane perfshim) `ccfp_intr_integer`, `ccfp_intr_integer_part` and `ccfp_intr_abs` of a float or a binary integer: libcob's result field, size, attribute and bytes (the 4 or 8 byte binary field `cob_alloc_field` makes; ABS keeps the argument's attribute, for a float whose decimal is exact in libcob's 96 digits, otherwise libcob's). Tests: `whole_numbers`, `absolute_values` (specials, 20 000 and 40 000 random floats, tiny ones past the 96 digits).
- [x] T59305 [US3-client, core] (lane perfshim) `FUNCTION INTEGER(a / b)` of two integers (WORLD-CELL, LIGHT-GET, GI-SOLID-CELL: every cell query): `ccfp_intr_binop` makes libcob's quotient field without GMP (the decimal of a * 10^38 / b cut toward zero, trailing zeros dropped, in the 4 or 8 byte binary field or the DISPLAY field of `cob_alloc_field`, with GMP's own count of the digits, the sign by `cob_real_put_sign`) from two divisions of 19 digits; `ccfp_intr_integer` and `ccfp_intr_integer_part` take the whole number of a quotient they made from its operands, which equals libcob's (no whole number lies between the cut quotient and a / b). Operands of 62 bits or more, a zero and an exact quotient stay as they were. Test: `integer_quotients` (edges and 40 000 random pairs of 1 to 62 bits, powers of two up to 2^40: size, attribute and bytes equal to libcob's, and INTEGER and INTEGER-PART of them).
- [x] T59306 [US3-client] (lane perfshim) `ccfp_intr_pi`: FUNCTION PI is libcob's field, kept after the first call (500 ns a call). A field with FUNCTION PI's digits inside a function argument (`ccfp_intr_binop`, SIN, COS, SQRT, TAN, ATAN) is read as the double nearest to pi: `FUNCTION COS(DEGREES * FUNCTION PI / 180)` is then a function of doubles like the SIN and COS of radians, in the class FPFAST already accepts. Tests: `pi_in_arguments`, `pi_field`.
- [x] T59307 [US3-client, core] (lane perfshim) `ccfp_intr_tan` and `ccfp_intr_atan`: libm's double within 1e-12 (relative) and 1e-15 of libcob's multi precision result for 20 000 random numbers; any argument this file does not read is libcob's. `FUNCTION ATAN` is 150 microseconds a call in libcob (264 000 ns measured under load). MOB-YAW-TOWARD already used libm's atan2 (`test_mob_yaw_toward_equiv`); the remaining 34 uses of FUNCTION ATAN (mob headings, arrows, dragon) now cost 23 ns, with the deviation of SIN and COS: libm's rounding of the last bit of a FLOAT-LONG result, which a FLOAT-SHORT result shows with probability about 1e-9 a call.
- [x] T59308 [US3-client, core] (lane perfshim) The shim's own helpers: `tag`, `tag_value`, `as_double` and `both_double` are always_inline and read and write the limb of a tagged decimal through the mpz structure (three GMP calls a float operation removed); `integer_of` reads a binary field without a byte loop. `tools/package.mk`: `FPFAST_INTRINSICS` gains tan atan integer integer_part abs pi and the new `FPFAST_PLAIN` list (cmp_llint cmp numeric_cmp get_int get_llint move), the same `-D` rename, the stamp counts all three lists.
- [x] T59309 [US3-client] (lane perfshim) `shim/tests/test_fpfast_idioms.cob`: the constructs of T59301 to T59307 as a COBOL program sees them, 28 cases that hold with FPFAST=1 and with FPFAST=0 (the values libcob alone gives, within 1e-9 where the function is libm's).
- [x] T59310 [US3-client] (lane perfshim) `tools/package.mk` gave clang no optimisation flag for the shim's C (`CFLAGS := -std=c11 -Wall -Wextra ...`), so `cc_fpfast.c` ran at -O0 in the release build too: the same code compiled with -O2 is 3 to 4 times faster (micro: a float expression 52 ns to 13, a float compare 15 to 3, FUNCTION SIN 24 to 12). The operator allowed the change: `ifeq ($(RELEASE)$(COVERAGE),10) CFLAGS += -O2 endif` after the CFLAGS line (shim and every package's C, release builds only; the cobc flags are untouched). End to end effect: to be measured after the rebuild (see T59312).

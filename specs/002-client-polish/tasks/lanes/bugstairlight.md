## Phase bugstairlight (lane bugstairlight, operator report of try19: "stairs render dark, which is the first real render issue I've seen this run"; follow-up clue: stairs lit by sky light are right, stairs lit only by block light (torches, lanterns, glowstone) are dark, chests and other partial blocks likewise; T62500-T62599)

Related closed work: bugblack (cells that let light through read the open sky), bugtorchblack (quads inside their cell take the light of their own cell, T42003).

- [x] T62500 [bugstairlight] Where the cause is: the core. The stand-in block light engine (`core/src/light.cob`: LIGHT-ADD, LIGHT-REMOVE, LIGHT-FRESH, LIGHT-BLOCK-CHANGED, and the LIGHT-SPREAD oracle) let light into a cell only when `CB-OPAQUE` of its block was 0, and `CB-OPAQUE` is 1 for every block that has a collision shape and occludes, so a stair, a slab, a path, farmland, snow, a carpet, a fence, a wall, a chest, a bed, a cauldron, a hopper, an anvil and the like stored block light 0 in their own cell and passed none on. The client's mesher is right (12 section 1.3 steps 2 and 3: a quad inside its cell takes the own cell, a border quad the neighbour cell it looks into; both channels, sky and block, come from the one cell read by `MESH-CELL`); what it read was 0, so the treads and risers of a stair, the posts of a fence and the faces of the neighbours that look into such a cell were drawn at the 0.1 floor wherever sky light does not reach (a room lit by torches, under a roof, at night). Sky light was never wrong for them: `LIGHT-SKY` writes the level that reached the cell (15 in the open) before it zeroes the cells below an occluder.

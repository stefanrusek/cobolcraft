## Lane 002-lane-bugsky (T30500+)

Operator report from the try13 play: "clouds, sun and moon don't render" (seen in a superflat world, true of every world with sky light). No existing open task covers it: the sky, fog and clouds were left out of 002 on purpose (spec FR-043, plan D-light, ticket CC-0007 still a draft), so the client never had a draw record for the sun, the moon, the stars or the clouds, only the clear colour of SKY-UPDATE. The dimension_type registry change of the conv1 lane (T13003: attributes, timelines and default_clock left out of the NBT) is not the cause: the client reads none of those fields and never did (the day, the sky colour and the weather come from Set Time and the Game Events; has_skylight, min_y and height from the registry, which are still sent).

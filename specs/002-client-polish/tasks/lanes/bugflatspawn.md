## Lane 002-lane-bugflatspawn (T58500-T58599; operator report on try16 db901a9a: "hostile mobs aren't spawning in my super flat world at night"; the sky looks right, stars and moon render)

Investigation (red first, in-process, no window). The night spawn pass itself works at db901a9a: a new superflat world (default `flat` preset, seed 12345, difficulty normal, hostile mobs on, extra dimensions on, creative player in the open, time 14000), through the core (`core/tests/acc_bugflatspawn_night.cob`, debug and RELEASE=1 builds, chunks preloaded or fed by the queue, a world reopened from its level.dat with the request cobolcraft) and through the client's own path (`tests/convergence/acc_bugflatspawn_client.cob`: create-world screen, SP-OPEN, SP-DEFAULTS settings, CL-STEP frames): 70 to 81 hostile mobs (zombie, skeleton, spider, creeper, witch...) after 600 to 1000 ticks, the first 24 within 20 ticks of dusk; the client's replica holds them with models. So lanes genlimits (STDMOB-SPOT-OK is not the natural spawner's rule: MS6-RULE-AT is), bugblack, bugsky/bugcelestial (CW-TIME is the only clock the spawner reads and the Set Time total is the same value), bugflatvillage, bugvlag (MS6-COLUMN-TOP) and bugwater did not break the night spawn of a fresh world. The bisect: the red test below fails the same way at try15 6789c8b5 (T58504), because the cause predates it.

Root cause (core, not client, not shim): `MOBS-TICK` (core/src/mobs.cob, T6102 `perf(core): mobs more than 64 blocks from every player are not stepped`, 7bd258f4, in try15) left a mob out of every player's 64 blocks out of its list, and `MS6-DESPAWN-TICK` is called only for the listed mobs. Doc 18 section 6.11: "`Entity.checkDespawn` is called ... for every entity ... before the check that skips entities outside the entity-ticking range (so mobs far from players are still despawn-checked each tick)". So a hostile mob 65 to 128 blocks away never random-despawned and one beyond 128 blocks (the player flew or walked off, a saved world reopened elsewhere) never despawned at all, and neither was stepped, so no undead burnt either. `MS6-CYCLE` counts every standard mob of the dimension for the global cap (`70 x chunks / 289`, 18 section 6.3), so the 70 mobs of one night (spawned 24 to 128 blocks out, most beyond 64) filled the monster cap for good: measured on the real path, after one night and a 2400 tick day 71 of 76 hostile mobs were still there, a second night added none, and a player taken 3000 blocks away got no hostile mob in 600 ticks while 81 stood 3000 blocks behind. A flat world (nothing to stop a walk, creative flight) makes it show at once.

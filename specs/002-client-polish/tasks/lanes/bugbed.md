## Lane 002-lane-bugbed (T58100 to T58199)

Operator reports from play-testing try16: "breaking the foot of a bed drops nothing and leaves the head half of the bed, same the other way, though breaking the head drops a bed head half, placing places only a foot"; "I can't sleep in beds"; "mobs stand on beds as if they are full blocks"; "path block in front of a door no longer blocks entry".

- [x] T58100 Bed halves (24 part 2 section 5 Beds row, 29 sections 2.2, 3.2, 9.5 and 10.4; core): a bed item placed a foot facing north and no head (no placement state for beds), a half whose partner was gone was never destroyed, and breaking the foot of a whole bed (village beds) dropped nothing because the loot table of a bed only drops with `part = head`. Now `PS-BED` (place-shape.cob, family 17 of PS-FAMILY: facing = the player's horizontal direction, refused unless the head cell is inside the border and empty or replaceable), `BED-PLACE-HEAD` (bed-block.cob, setPlacedBy: the head half one cell along the facing), support rule 21 (support-29.cob: a bed half survives while the other half stands, so the update engine destroys an orphan with its drops and the loot of the head half drops the item once whichever half was broken), `BED-CREATIVE-BREAK` (playerWillDestroy: a creative foot takes its head silently, no item). Test: `core/tests/acc_bed_halves.cob` (red before: 19 of 95 cases passed; all 16 colours and the straw bed place, break in survival and creative, packets to the clients for both cells, head cell taken, water and grass in the head cell, a second use, a bed beside a bed).
- [x] T58101 Sleeping (33 sections 5.1 to 5.3, 53 pose SLEEPING, 05a Animate action 0, 05c Player Command 0 STOP_SLEEPING, 18 entry 14; core): the use of a bed set a server flag and nothing else: no pose, no sleeping position, no wake up, no reason for a refusal, STOP_SLEEPING ignored, a bed whose head half was missing slept in all the same, the flag of a player who left asleep stayed on the row. Now `WORLDSIM-SLEEP-USE` moves to the head half (a lone foot is consumed with no effect), refuses an occupied bed, checks the cells above both halves, sets the respawn point at the head half and says why a refusal happened as an overlay line (`block.minecraft.bed.too_far_away`, `obstructed`, `no_sleep`, `not_safe`, `occupied`); `SLEEP-BEGIN` / `SLEEP-WAKE` / `SLEEP-FORGET` (sleep-bed.cob) send pose 2 and the sleeping position (Set Entity Data entry 14) to the viewers and to the player, mark both halves occupied, send Animate wake up and the standing pose on STOP_SLEEPING (`MOVE-SPRINT`), the morning and the night skip, and clear the row when the player leaves (`PLAYER-LEAVE`). Test: `core/tests/acc_bed_sleep.cob`; `core/tests/acc_bed_spawn.cob` now uses a whole bed.
- [x] T58102 Entities rest on the collision shape, not the cell (51 block shapes, 41 section 3, 18 section 3.2; core): `ENTITY-BOX-HITS` (ground items, mobs, boats, minecarts) asked `GI-SOLID-CELL` (the `CB-SOLID` flag of the block) so every solid block was a full cube and a body rested at the cell's top. Same root for a path block (0.9375), farmland (0.9375), soul sand (0.875), a bottom slab (0.5) and the other part-height blocks. Test: `core/tests/test_entity_shapes.cob`. Not changed: blocks without `CB-SOLID` that have a collision shape (carpets) stay passable for entities, and `STDMOB-CELL` (path finding) still counts a part-height block as a solid floor.

## Lane 002-lane-doorpath (T6900+)

Operator report on the release build: "when the ground in front of a door is a dirt_path block, the player cannot walk through the door; jumping and pressing forward gets through".

- [x] T6900 Step-up under a lintel (13 section 1.4, 51 section 6: a move blocked horizontally but passable when the entity rises by at most the step height is resolved by stepping up): PHYS-STEP-UP (`client/src/physics.cob`) rose by the full 0.6 from where the player stood, and a player on a dirt_path (feet 1/16 below the full floor of the doorway cell) then had its head (1.75) above the lintel of a two high door, so the move into the doorway at that height hit the lintel and the 1/16 step was refused; jumping got over it because the jump rises first and moves later. The prediction was at fault, not the core (the server only refuses a position whose box overlaps a collision shape; a step of 1/16 and a drop of 1/16 are no overlap). The rise is now the smaller of the rise at the start and the rise the box can make at the cell the move enters (the box swept toward the wanted move), so the lintel leaves 0.3125 and the player steps over the lip. A step with nothing overhead is unchanged. Test: `client/tests/test_physics_doorpath.cob` (red before: an open door after a dirt_path approach, the player stopped at the lip; green: it walks through; an open door over stone and over dirt_path, a dirt_path cell with no door pass; a closed door over stone, over dirt_path and after a dirt_path approach still stops the player).

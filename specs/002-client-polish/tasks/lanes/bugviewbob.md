## Phase bugviewbob (lane bugviewbob, play-test bug of try19: "view bobbing is now more obviously missing than ever"; T62400-T62499)

Root cause (client, not core or shim): nothing in the client tree draws view bobbing or the hurt and death tilt. hand.cob's header lists "view bobbing, sway and the hurt tilt (6.1)" as not done, the player keeps no walk distance or bob amount, and RENDER-LIST-BUILD builds the world view from yaw and pitch alone. With the smooth per-frame interpolation of lane bugsmooth the camera now glides in every frame, which makes the missing walk sway more obvious. Lane p70g (unmerged, branch 002-lane-p70g, T47100 and T47101) built it on top of the whole p70 hand pose chain; this lane ports only the view part onto try19 (doc 12 section 6.1 steps 1 and 2, 53 section 15.2, 57-1 section 5), as one program `VIEW-POSE` that the world view and the hand both start from. Hand sway (12 section 6.1 step 3, 53 section 15.3) is not implemented (it needs the lagging view angles and the hand pose chain of p70).

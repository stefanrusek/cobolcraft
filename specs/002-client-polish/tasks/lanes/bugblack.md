## Lane 002-lane-bugblack (T33600+)

Operator report from the try14 play, left open by lane bugblocks (T32002): black squares on the grass floor, in diagonal chains and X shapes, textures at the 0.1 brightness floor (0.1 x the grass colour), i.e. faces whose looked-into cell reads sky light 0 and block light 0. Base of the lane: 002-lane-bugblocks 9c949be4 (try14 plus a test).

- [x] T33600 [US3] (lane bugblack) Root cause found, in the core (not the client, not the shim): the stand-in sky light (`core/src/light.cob` LIGHT-SKY, fed by WORLD-COLUMN-TOPS) stored 15 above a column's topmost non-void block and 0 at and below it, whatever that block was, so the cell of a plant, a path, a layer of snow, the leaves of a tree or the top of the water read sky 0 (docs/protocol/32 section 5.2 and 24 part 2 section 5.3: sky light passes through a block of dampening 0 and a block of dampening 1 takes one level). Measured on a standard world (seed 12345, view distance 2) before the fix: every plant cell of the 9 chunks around the spawn read 0 (30 of 30) and so did the top of the water (911 of 911); cells built above the terrain (short grass, dirt path, leaves, water) read 0 where 15, 15, 14 and 14 are right.
- [x] T33601 [US3] (lane bugblack) Red first: `core/tests/test_light_plants.cob` (real data, CC_GENERATED_DIR; skipped as a pass without it): a standard world's spawn chunk is generated and lit; every generated cell of a plant, dirt path, leaf or water with open sky above is read from the stored light (plants and paths 15, topmost leaf and topmost water 14, the air under the topmost leaf 13), and the same blocks are built above the terrain with WORLD-SET-BLOCK (the block change relights the chunk): short grass 15, dirt path 15 and 0 under it, leaf 14 with 13 and 12 under it, a leaf under a leaf 13, water 14. 8 of its 14 cases failed before the fix (the 7 built-cell cases and the generated water).
- [x] T33602 [US3] (lane bugblack) Fix: `core/copy/CBLOCKS.cpy` CB-S-DAMP (the sky light dampening of each state), filled by BLOCKS-FILL with the rules of L32-BLOCK-RULES (light-32e.cob: leaves 1, tinted glass 15, liquids 1, full opaque cube 15, else 0 when the state propagates sky light down, else 1) from the class, the outline shape (BSH-OUT; BLOCKS-OPEN builds the shapes before the blocks now) and the fluid (waterlogged, liquids, seagrass and kelp); `core/src/light.cob` LIGHT-SKY walks each column from its topmost non-void block: 15 through dampening 0, the level less the dampening (at least 1) after the first cell that takes some, 0 below a cell that occludes (CB-OPAQUE; a slab or a path still reads the level that reached it). The only place that decides the sky top in the core: chunk streaming sends the stored light, the block-change relight and chunk lighting both call LIGHT-SKY, the heightmaps (WORLD-COLUMN-TOPS) are unchanged. The light engine of 32 (lane p46d) is not touched.
- [x] T33603 [US3-client] (lane bugblack) `client/tests/test_mesher_plants.cob`: the mesh brightness of faces that look into a non-opaque cell (a top face and a side face beside a lucite cell): 1.00 and 0.60 for stored sky 0 (the client's stopgap, which treats only a full cube as the surface, assumes 15) and for stored 15, 0.93 and 0.56 for stored 14, never the 0.10 floor. Green before and after: the client was correct; its stopgap only assumes light where the core stored 0, which is what the core did.
- [ ] T33604 [US3] (lane bugblack) NOT DONE, left to the light engine lane (p46d): sideways spreading of sky light (a stored cell under the eaves of a roof or the edge of a canopy still reads the column value, 0 under a roof), face-shape occlusion of the sky column by slabs and stairs, and the retirement of the LIGHT-RAW and LIGHT-SKY-SPREAD workarounds of the mob rules (they still give the right answer: they only act where the stored value is 0).

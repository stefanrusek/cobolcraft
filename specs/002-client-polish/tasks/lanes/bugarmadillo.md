## Lane 002-lane-bugarmadillo (T60600+)

Operator report (try18, savanna): armadillos do not curl into balls when he runs around them, and a hit one freezes without any roll animation. Base: origin/002-client-polish db901a9a.

- [x] T60600 [US15] (lane bugarmadillo) Root cause of "running around them does nothing", in the core: `ARMADILLO-TICK` (core/src/mob-19-13.cob) scanned for danger once every 80 ticks (the 19 section 13 constant SCARE_CHECK_INTERVAL stood in for the sensor), so a sprinting player was noticed only when a 4-second scan tick happened to fall while he was inside 7 x 2 x 7. The sensor `armadillo_scare_detected` (44 part 1 RQ-1.34, 44 part 4 section 1.5) scans every 5 ticks with a memory of 80 ticks. Also missing from isScaredBy (44 part 4 section 12): a spectator never scares and a passenger does.
- [x] T60601 [US15] (lane bugarmadillo) Red first: `core/tests/test_mob_19_13_scare.cob` (real Player Command packets and CORE-TICK): walking, sneaking and a sprinting spectator never scare; a passenger scares within 5 ticks; a sprinting player 3 blocks away rolls it to state 1 within 5 ticks and the Set Entity Data goes to the viewer; the state stays scared while he keeps running; after he stops it unrolls (state 3 seen) and is idle again, each change sent. 4 of its 10 cases failed before the fix (spectator, passenger, the 5-tick roll, the viewer packet; the later cases passed because their own 80-tick scan came in time).
- [x] T60602 [US15] (lane bugarmadillo) Fix: scan interval 5, `PL-MODE 3` excluded, `EN-VEHICLE` of the player's entity counts as a passenger (core/src/mob-19-13.cob). The hurt path (ARMADILLO-HURT: danger 80 and state 1 with Set Entity Data) and the undead rule were already right and are unchanged (test_mob_19_13 green).
- [x] T60603 [US3-client] (lane bugarmadillo) Root cause of "it freezes but the animation does not run", in the client: the core sends ARMADILLO_STATE (entry 18, serializer 36) at every transition, but the replica ignored it, `ENTITY-POSE-MORE` fed the armadillo animator the stopped start ticks and no shell flag, and Entity Event 64 (peek) was dropped. The models (34-5 10, `EF5-ARMADILLO-*`) were in the base but never wired.

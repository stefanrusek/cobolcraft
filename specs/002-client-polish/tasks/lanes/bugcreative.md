## Phase bugcreative (lane bugcreative, operator reports of try15 about the bedrock of the Nether roof; T45000-T45199)

Two reports: "creative mode can't break bedrock" and "mobs are spawning on bedrock". Both causes are in the core; the client needed no change.

- [x] T45000 [bugcreative] Root cause of "creative mode can't break bedrock" (core, `BLOCK-ACTION` in `core/src/breaking.cob`): the hardness test (`WS-HARDNESS < 0`, "never broken") came before the game mode test, so a creative player's Start Destroy Block on bedrock, barrier, end portal frame, structure block, jigsaw, command blocks and light blocks was refused and the block sent back. Docs 13 section 2.5 and 29 section 10.4 say the server breaks at once on Start when the player has `instabuild`, whatever the hardness. The client (`CONTROLS-INPUT`) sends Start Destroy Block for any targeted block in creative and has no hardness gate (survival keeps its break time -1, `CONTROLS-BREAK-TIME`); no client change.
- [x] T45001 [bugcreative] `BREAK-CREATIVE` (`core/src/breaking.cob`, new): the creative break of 29 section 10.4 in order: the held sword, trident or mace cannot destroy blocks in creative (`BK-CAN-DESTROY`; the block stays and the player is sent its state), a game master block (command blocks, structure block, jigsaw, test blocks) needs creative and the game master permission (`BR-SOURCE-LEVEL` 2 or more; `BK-DESTROY`), then `PIGLIN-GUARDED-BREAK`, Level Event 2001 with the old state for everybody but the breaker (`LEVEL-EVENT`), the removal (`SUP-DESTROY-STATE`: the water of a waterlogged block or a water plant stays) and `DOUBLE-BREAK` (both halves of a door or tall plant); no item, no tool wear, no exhaustion. `BLOCK-ACTION` calls it before the hardness test; survival and adventure still refuse hardness -1.
- [x] T45002 [bugcreative] `BREAK-FINISH` (survival break): sends the same Level Event 2001 to the other players (29 section 10.4 step 5) and leaves the water of a waterlogged block (it set the cell to air before).
- [x] T45003 [bugcreative] Docs design `standin-content.md` section 1.1 and the section 3 line "Breaking sets the position to state 0": the baseplate no longer says "nobody may break it": survival and adventure refuse it, creative breaks it (operator decision of try15, 13 section 2.5).
- [x] T45010 [bugcreative] Test `core/tests/acc_us4_break_unbreakable.cob` (superflat standard world, real data, two players): creative breaks bedrock, barrier, end portal frame, reinforced deepslate, light block, structure block, jigsaw and the three command blocks on Start Destroy Block with no item on the ground; a creative player who is no game master cannot break the structure block, jigsaw or command blocks (they stay) but breaks bedrock; a sword, a trident or a mace in the hand breaks neither bedrock nor stone, a pickaxe breaks bedrock; survival and adventure cannot break bedrock (not on Start, not on Stop); both halves of a door go; a waterlogged slab leaves a water source; the other player gets the Level Event 2001 of a creative and of a survival break and the breaker does not. Red before: 12 of 17 cases failed (the break cases of every hardness -1 block, the permission-free bedrock case, the Level Event).
- [x] T45011 [bugcreative] `core/tests/acc_us4_break_creative.cob`: the old edge case "the baseplate is never broken" is split: a survival player cannot break it, a creative player breaks it (the hardness -1 stand-in block of y = 0).
- [x] T45012 [bugcreative] Convergence test `tests/convergence/acc_creative_bedrock.cob` (client controls, memory connection and core in one process): aiming the look ray at bedrock, a barrier, an end portal frame and a structure block and holding the left button leaves it in the core and the client's replica in survival and removes it from both in creative. Passes with the core fix and no client change (the client has no hardness gate in creative).
- [x] T45100 [bugcreative] Root cause of "mobs are spawning on bedrock" (core, `MS6-STURDY-TOP` in `core/src/mob-18-6.cob`): a natural spawn needs a floor that passes `Block.isValidSpawn` (top face sturdy, light below 14) and the core kept only the sturdy-top part by key names; the blocks that set the predicate to `never` were not modelled, so bedrock (a full block with a sturdy top) was a valid floor. Docs: 44-residual-mob-questions-1 RQ-1.10 lists the 35 `isValidSpawn` call sites: `never` for `bedrock`, `glass`, `moving_piston`, the wood, iron, crimson, warped and copper trapdoors, `barrier`, `scaffolding`, `chorus_flower`, `tinted_glass`, the 16 stained glass blocks and the copper grates. Docs 18 section 6.4 chooses y in [minY, surface height + 1] and nothing in 18, 20 or 37 limits natural spawns to the dimension's `logical_height` (37-1: `logical_height` limits portals and the chorus teleport only; 37-6 section 6: "no player boundary at the bedrock roof"), so the cell at y 128 above the roof is a candidate position; what keeps a mob off the roof is the bedrock floor, not a height rule.
- [x] T45101 [bugcreative] `MS6-STURDY-TOP`: the "never" list above (bedrock, glass, tinted and stained glass, barrier, moving piston, scaffolding, chorus flower, `_trapdoor` keys, copper grate) is never a floor.
- [x] T45110 [bugcreative] Test `core/tests/test_mob_18_6_bedrock.cob` (real data, `MS6-VALID-SPOT` with the zombie rule at night under open sky, 200 seeds): a zombie spawns on stone and obsidian and on none of bedrock, glass, tinted glass, stained glass, barrier, scaffolding and copper grate. Red before: bedrock, glass, tinted glass, stained glass, barrier and copper grate passed 45 of 200 seeds.
- [x] T45111 [bugcreative] Test `core/tests/test_mob_18_6_nether_roof.cob` (standard world, extra dimensions on, placeholder Nether terrain: bedrock floor y 0 and roof y 127): a player on the roof (y 129) and then in the cavity, 400 ticks each with every new mob recorded and removed: no mob at y 128 or above, none with a bedrock block below it, and mobs do spawn in the cavity. Red before: 12 mobs spawned on the roof.

## Lane 002-lane-bugregress (T49500+)

Four tests reported failing on the unchanged try15 base 6789c8b5 (batch12 8f42338b plus the merges of bughand, bugblack, buglocate, buglocate2, genlimits and wallitems). Each was bisected by building the test at 8f42338b and then after each merge in the try15 order (a scratch branch, `git merge` one lane at a time; the genlimits merge conflicts in core/src/mob-20-4.cob and was resolved with the try15 file). All four are old fixtures made invalid by an intended change or by an earlier one; no code regression, so only tests changed.

- [x] T49500 [US12] (lane bugregress) `core/tests/acc_us12_worldsim_play.cob` cases 'US12 29a 2' (two cases): first bad merge bugblack (97436509, sky light by the dampening of the column's cells). The test's hand-installed `minecraft:water` had no CB-S-DAMP (0), so its cells read the open sky 15 like air and a placed or flowing water source changed no stored light, hence no Light Update (49) in the packet lists. With the real water (dampening 1, cell 14 under the open sky) the placement and each flowing cell do change the light and the Light Updates are correct: INSTALL-WATER now sets CB-S-DAMP 1. Expectations unchanged.
- [x] T49501 [US15] (lane bugregress) `core/tests/test_mob_21_3.cob` 'glow squid: y <= sea - 33, brightness 0, water at the position passes': first bad merge bugblack. The fixture's water column from sea - 40 to sea - 30 had open air above it (the test world has no terrain there), so with sky light passing through water (32 section 5.2) the cell reads 14 and the glow squid rule rightly refuses. The column now runs to sea - 28 and is closed by a stone at sea - 27, so the cell is dark (the 'above sea - 33 fails' case at sea - 29 now really tests the depth limit, with water at the position). The sea level is unchanged by genlimits for this world (DM-SEA-LEVEL / DIM-SEA-LEVEL-OF agree in the test world).
- [x] T49502 [US15] (lane bugregress) `core/tests/test_stdmob_extra.cob` 'no spawn in a dark cell': first bad merge genlimits (14e93a23, STDMOB-SPOT-OK reads the sky light through LIGHT-SKY-SPREAD). The 'dark cell' was a grass cell with two cells of air above in a column of the test world that has no terrain there (stored sky 0 only because a grass block 40 cells up still roofs the column): the neighbouring cells are air under open sky, and the engine's sideways spreading gives the cell 14, so an animal may spawn there. The fixture now builds a real dark cell: stone on the four sides of the two air cells and a cap above. Expectation unchanged.
- [x] T49503 [US5] (lane bugregress) `tests/convergence/acc_us5_inventory_throw.cob`: fails the same way on batch12 8f42338b itself (not a try15 regression, not wallitems or placehead; the inventory throw path of core is unchanged). Cause: since T4600 (bae3d00c, docs/protocol/40-1 section 5.5 mouse down and up) a press on a slot with an empty cursor acts at once (PICKUP) and its release is swallowed, wherever it happens, so the old sequence 'press on slot 1, move outside, release' only picks the loam up (the cursor keeps 64) and nothing is thrown. The test now picks up with a click on the slot and throws with a click outside the window (press with the stack starts a drag, the release of an empty drag drops the stack, PICKUP at -999), which the 40-1 rules and client/src/screens/pointer.cob specify. Case name 'drags' became 'clicks'.

## Lane 002-lane-bugvillage (T30200+)

Operator report (try13 play): in a new superflat world, near a village, zombies attacked the villagers, spiders stood on roofs and creepers appeared. Reproduced by `core/tests/acc_bugvillage_spawns.cob` (superflat seed 12345, its first village generated round a creative player, the natural spawner run 300 ticks by day and 300 by night, every spawn recorded). Findings: zombies hunting villagers is the documented behaviour (`MOBVS-TICK`, 20 section 4 and 13), a plains village generates no hostile mob, there is no zombie siege in the core (44-3 section 1.7 is not carried), and no pillager raid is involved. The defect is the light: the hostile mobs that appeared by day stood in cells the stored sky light reads 0 (light.cob keeps 15 above a column's topmost block and 0 at and below it, no sideways spreading), so the cells under the eaves of every house and porch counted as dark.

Operator bug (try15, fixed by lane bugtrade for the merchant window but hidden behind it): villagers in generated villages never take a profession. After the merge of the p49 villager brain (batch15, ea2db22b) core test `acc_bugtrade_village` failed again: the village's single villager never moved and never claimed the job site 14 blocks away, then crashed at TRY-EACH (no villager with a profession).

- [x] T57000 [US15-core] Root cause (core, not the guess VL-ACQ-OK itself): the generated village's villager stands in a stone house whose only way out is a closed `oak_door`. `VL-CAN-REACH` (the path check of AcquirePoi, 44-3 section 2.1 step 6, new with p49p/p49q) searches with `STDMOB-PATH`, whose cells (`STDMOB-CELL`) read any block with a collision shape as solid, a door included, so the search ended at the wall (3 cells, `reached` 0) and every beat the candidate was turned away and the retry delay grew (49, 107, 152, 198 ticks, `TM-ACQ-DELAY-JOB`); `pot` stayed 0, so no claim, no walk, no profession. (Before the p49 brain there was no path check; with the check forced to succeed the villager claims at once but is then stopped by the same door: the walk and the physics took a door for a wall too.) Found by instrumenting a copy of the test (POI index filled at tick 150, the site read at (325,-59,-173), `VL-CAN-REACH` failing with budgets 100, 256 and 1000, the cells around the villager listed with their block keys).
- [x] T57001 [US15-core] A villager opens wooden doors (18 section 5.1: path type WALKABLE_DOOR for a door a mob opens by hand, DOOR_IRON_CLOSED stays a wall; 44-3 section 2.15 InteractWithDoor).

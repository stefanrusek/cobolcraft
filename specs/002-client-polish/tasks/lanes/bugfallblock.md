## Phase bugfallblock (lane bugfallblock, operator report of try15: "falling blocks still delete torches rather than breaking"; T42200-T42399)

Operator report: sand or gravel falling onto or through a torch makes the torch vanish; it neither breaks nor drops. Reference: docs/protocol/29b part 1 sections 3.1 (`isFree`, the fall tick) and 4.3 to 4.5 (landing, the item drop); 24 part 1 section 10.1 (the replaceable flag).

- [x] T42200 [bugfallblock] Root cause 1 (core, `FB-STEP`, mob-21-8.cob): the landing test took "the cell is replaceable" from `FLUID-CELL`'s `holds` (a block without a collision shape), so a torch (or any thin block) in the landing cell counted as replaceable and `LEVEL-SET-BLOCK` overwrote it with the falling block: the torch was deleted, with no drop and no item. 29b-1 4.3 step 4 reads `canBeReplaced` (the registry flag: air, short grass, water, fire ...), and step 6 says that a block that cannot be placed is discarded and its own item drops (with the rule `entity_drops`) while the block in the cell stays. `FB-STEP` also passed the constant "drop" and "rule" flags to `GB-LAND`; it now reads `entity_drops`.
- [x] T42201 [bugfallblock] `FB-CELL-FREE` (new, mob-21-8.cob): `replaceable` (`CB-REPLACEABLE`, air or a liquid block) and `free` (`FallingBlock.isFree`: air, fire or soul fire, a liquid block, or replaceable) of a cell, the second through `GB-IS-FREE`. `FB-DROP-ITEM` (new): `spawnAtLocation` of the falling block's item (4.5), shared by the landing drop (`GB-LAND` action 5) and the expiry drop (4.4).
- [x] T42202 [bugfallblock] Root cause 2 (core, `GB-FALL-TICK`, growth-29b-1.cob): the cell below counted as free when it had no collision, so a sand or gravel block resting on a torch (or a rail, button ...) fell through it and then landed in its cell (root cause 1). 3.1 ST: the block falls only when the cell below is `isFree`; it now asks `FB-CELL-FREE`.
- [x] T42203 [bugfallblock] Test `core/tests/test_falling_block_standard.cob` (superflat world of the generated standard set, skipped as a pass without CC_GENERATED_DIR, real tick path): sand falling onto a standing torch (torch stays, one sand item, no entity left); sand resting on a torch stays; gravel onto a rail (rail stays, gravel item); gravel onto short grass (replaced, gravel placed, no grass item, no gravel item); an anvil onto a wall torch's cell (torch stays, anvil item); a wall torch on a falling sand block pops and drops a torch item, the sand lands; a torch standing on a falling sand block pops and drops its item, the sand lands; a column of two gravel blocks lands stacked; sand over fire, water and lava falls and takes the cell. Red before (8 of 34 cases failed: the torch, the rail and the wall torch were replaced, the sand over the torch fell, no items), green after (45 cases).
- [x] T42204 [bugfallblock] Checked, no change needed: attached blocks that lose their support to a falling block (wall torch on sand, torch on sand) already pop with their item (`SUP-OPEN`, `BLOCK-DROPS`: lane wallitems); a replaced block (short grass) correctly drops nothing (4.3 step 5b is `setBlockAndUpdate`, not a break), so the report's "destroyed block drops its loot" holds only for the supported attached blocks, not for a block the falling entity overwrites.
- [ ] T42210 [bugfallblock] Open, data (not fixed here, `tools/gen` is outside this lane): the generated block table gives the fifteen flowers (poppy, dandelion ... lily of the valley) no `noCollision` flag. `tools/gen/gen_tables.py` reads the "other settings" prose of 24 part 2 section 5 only for rows whose first cell names blocks in backticks; the row "140 to 154 the fifteen flowers" (no collision, instabreak, offset XZ, POPPED) has none, so `content/copy/gen/blocks.cpy` rows of the flowers have empty flags. In the core the flowers are solid (`CB-SOLID` 1): a falling block, an item or a mob rests on a poppy as on a full block (a gravel block landing on a poppy rests on top of it, no entity can walk through it). The same gap probably applies to any other row of that table that gives no backticked key; the replaceable flag is also not set for them (correct: flowers are not replaceable).
